## Cross-cutting findings
1. **Missing data is re-weighted away in every scorecard** (tech, option, stock score, fit `wavg`). A name missing its weakest component scores higher than one that reports it. That's gap #7, now confirmed everywhere. Fix: carry coverage/confidence (fund already does) and gate on it.
2. **Only fundamentals is peer-relative.** Technicals are self-relative; options are per contract. For a "relatively compare all stocks" framework, technicals need a cross-sectional (peer/universe) percentile view alongside the self-relative one.
3. **No scorecard has usable history yet** (fund snapshots start today; tech has none). Point-in-time snapshots for tech/options per the A14 contract are a prerequisite for Step 4 factor research, besides the reconstructable LSEG/price history.
4. Analyst signals live in two places: inside fund growth (IBES) and in the fit model's STREET (LSEG ARM / drift / predicted surprise). Measured overlap is low (Spearman 0.14–0.19); see `street_scenarios.md`.
