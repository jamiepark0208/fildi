## Verdict — RULE (shortlist = top/bottom k GICS industries by 60-session and 1-session median return ∪ top M single-name |move|/σ) FAIL as a narrowing filter
- Lift over a random shortlist of the same size: 0.99–1.12 for every view. Sector momentum, today's sector move and today's movers do not concentrate the next 21 sessions' top-decile names.
- Independent: 687 sessions, but 21-session forward windows overlap, so about 33 independent windows; both halves agree.
- So-what for the funnel: do not cut stocks by sector or intraday moves. Model-price the full universe (~600–900 names, daily bars + iv30, $0) and narrow only before real chains, by tested edge scores (S100, put edge, S054/S109/S172/S173 rules). Sectors stay as context and as concentration caps, not as a filter.
- winner-pass: n/a (a recall test of a filter, not a trade rule; there are no trades to dissect).
