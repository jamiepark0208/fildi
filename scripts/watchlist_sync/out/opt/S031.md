# S031 — overnight vs daytime drops (2026-10-15)

1631 ≥2σ 5-day drops, 548 stocks, 2025-07-24 → 2026-09-17 (halves split at 2026-03-03). Short sample: opens exist only from 2025-04.

| Drop made mostly… | n | 5d excess pp | 20d excess pp | got back to pre-drop price ≤20d vs base |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| overnight | 356 | -0.2 | +0.7 | 19% vs 19% |
| intraday | 1075 | +1.5 | +1.8 | 30% vs 21% |
| mixed | 200 | -0.4 | -0.0 | 20% vs 20% |

## Pre-registered tests (3, Holm, week-cluster bootstrap)
| Test | Effect | halves | Holm p | pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| overnight − intraday · 5-day excess | -1.7 pp | -0.7 / -2.9 | 0.017 | **PASS** |
| overnight − intraday · 20-day excess | -1.1 pp | +0.1 / -2.5 | 0.229 | no |
| overnight − intraday · recovery vs base | -9.5 pp | -5.9 / -13.3 | 0.012 | **PASS** |

Overnight = Σ log(open ÷ previous close) over the 5 drop days; intraday = Σ log(close ÷ open). Excess = minus the stock's mean forward return in that half; recovery base = share of the stock's days that climbed the same % within 20 days. Caveats: ~14 months of events (Jul 2025 → Sep 2026; 65-day warm-up after the 2025-04 data start), one market regime; the 5-day effect only just clears its 1.5 pp bar and the 20-day gap does not pass. Mechanism fits news gaps (earnings, guidance) being repriced for good, while intraday selling is more often flow that reverts.
