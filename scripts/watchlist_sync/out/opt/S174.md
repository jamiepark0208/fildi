## Setup
- Harness = S100 long-call panel (lab_tech universe, 721 names, 387 weeks), S081 pricer: 0.50Δ ~45 DTE call held to expiry, return per $ debit over T-bill, model-priced at own IV.
- FIT 2020-02 → 2024-06 (259 weeks), HOLDOUT 2024-07 → (128 weeks). Top-10 per week.
- Growth inputs, all point-in-time:
  - **Revenue YoY, acceleration, acceleration streak:** SEC companyfacts in vendor_raw, first-filed value, used only after its `filed` date. Q4 = FY − Q1..Q3. Covers 632 names, 366/week in holdout.
  - **Beat streak and 4-report average surprise:** earnings_calendar, 56 names/week in holdout (thin).
  - **IBES:** FY1→FY2 growth, 3-month revision, SUE.
- Directions fixed in advance (more growth → buy calls). Comparators: buy every name the same weeks (holdout +32.2 %) and the live S100 rule (ibes_drift + ret60: FIT +35.9 %, holdout +69.6 %, which reproduces S100).
- Independent events: 128 holdout weekly entries with ~45-day holds ≈ 20 non-overlapping windows. Block t uses 6-week blocks.
- Bug fixed during the run: the s085 loader drops IBES keys not on its list, so ibes_fwd_growth came out empty. Added the key and re-ran that one factor only.

## Verdict — RULE NO-LIFT (top-10 calls on S100 + growth factor)
- **RULE: weekly top-10 0.50Δ 45 DTE calls held to expiry, ranked on (S100 revisions + 60-day return) + one growth factor → NO-LIFT** over S100 for all 8 factors, ~20 independent windows (underpowered for small gains).
- **Beat streak and surprise average HURT** the S100 rule: −38 / −31 pts, t −2.4 / −2.0, both halves negative.
- Revenue acceleration looks good in the holdout (+32 to +42 pts) but loses in FIT (−3 to −20) and fails a half. It's an era effect, not a rule.
- Alone, FY1→FY2 growth and 3-month revisions beat buying every name (t 2.3 / 2.6), but neither beats S100 (+70 %). Revisions are already in S100.
- **So-what:** keep the DCF and the call rule as they are. Growth is already priced through revisions and momentum. Don't add a growth bonus to call picks.
