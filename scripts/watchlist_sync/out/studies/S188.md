## Rule tested
Entry: sell a listed weekly put 3–9 calendar days before expiry (first LSEG quote day, fill = bid + 23 % of spread) · exit: hold to expiry, intrinsic at the expiry close · structure: single short put · selection: rank within the week by the name's own past-52-week simulated put P&L (option momentum) or breach rate.

## Independent events
Unit = week (names co-move). Down cell 78 weeks (2,319 puts), up cell 204 weeks, all 279. ≥ 30 in every cell, so a fail is a fail, not UNDERPOWERED. Same stocks and calendar as S187, so this is a robustness check, not fresh data.

Option momentum doesn't separate winners from losers at all. Income is partly mechanical (a bigger premium is a bigger kept return). Two traits aren't mechanical: winners were **deeper off their high** (matches S109's ≥ 25 % rule) and had **tight spreads** (10 % vs 31 % of the fill). **Next rule:** in down tape, add spread ÷ fill as a within-week rank on S109 real puts (registered sign −, after income/OTM/drawdown). Spread is a cost we can see before entry; S187's simulator couldn't.
