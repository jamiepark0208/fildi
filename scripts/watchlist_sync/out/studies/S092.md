## Known weaknesses

- Fiscal period end is the availability date. LSEG can still revise the newest quarter; older quarters matched a single-period refetch.
- Country rank (EQ, short interest, insider) is not the region rank used for combined alpha. On a US book they are close; Smart Holdings is the region rank.
- Week-clustered lag 3 would treat ~13 identical weeks as independent. Lag 13 is the amendment.
- Field codes that returned error 218 may exist under a name this probe did not try.
