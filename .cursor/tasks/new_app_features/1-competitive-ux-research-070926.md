### Resolution
Benchmarked 10 comparable products (StockTwits, Covers.com, Barchart, OptionStrat, TipRanks, Koyfin, TradingView, Seeking Alpha, Finviz, Robinhood) and concluded TradeDash's gap is product packaging, not backend depth: missing a "start here" home surface, inline score explanations, curated daily picks, trust/track-record signals, and progressive disclosure. Positioned TradeDash as "Covers.com for put sellers." Spawned the prioritized feature list (first-run tour, contextual score tooltips, home-as-Today redesign, scanner simple mode, top-picks feed, analyst leaderboard, command palette, etc.), tracked as individual new_app_features task files in INDEX.md.
