## Claude's view (LOG C2, scored)
**Up by year-end, modestly: ~65 % that SPY closes 2026 above 769.36, central case +2 to +4 %, but ~30 % odds of a ≥ 5 % dip first**
(vs 17 % base) because a December hike lands on a CAPE > 30 / 10y ≈ 5 % market. Leadership: **equal-weight beats cap-weight to
year-end (~60 %)** — the spenders' FCF problem doesn't go away by December (2027 capex goes *up*), while the rest of the market is
already rotating (RSP +5.8 % vs QQQ −3.3 % in a month). Suppliers (chips, memory, power) hold up better than the spenders unless C fires.
What I disagree with Jamie on: the earnings were not poor — operations accelerated; the cash cost of AI is what the market is pricing.
