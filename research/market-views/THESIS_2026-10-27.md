## History: months that looked like today (1954–2022)

12 nearest analog months (one per episode): **2000-05, 2021-08, 2018-08**, 1997-01, 2017-03, 1999-04, 2005-03, 2006-05, 2007-10, 2003-11, 2014-04, 2020-01.
→ 12 m median **+10.4 %**, up **75 %**, but **P(≥ 10 % drawdown) 42 %** (≈ 2× base), P(≥ 20 %) 8 %.

## Claude's view (scored like Jamie's, LOG C1)
Bullish on the **index** over 12 months, ~70 %, because earnings are doing the work (forward P/E ~20, not 1999).
But the path is likely bumpy: ~45 % odds of a ≥ 10 % drop first, because the one historically bad combination (very high CAPE + fast-rising 10y) is in place.
Names: cash-funded megacaps over debt-funded neoclouds (claim 10); **MP over USAR** (DoD 15 % stake + $110/kg NdPr floor protects MP's earnings; Commerce's USAR deal is a letter of intent at $17.17 and USAR trades $10.40); defense-tech upside depends on the $350 B reconciliation piece, which the midterm odds make less likely.

## What it means for the put book
- Puts lose in drawdowns, not in flat markets. A 45 % chance of a ≥ 10 % dip is the risk to manage, even with a bullish view.
- Keep the put band on **Auto**; don't shift closer on conviction. The engine already moves closer after a sharp drop and pauses in a panic (S101); macro timing failed (S087, S027).
- Signposts to watch (each moves the scenario weights): hyperscaler capex guides (this week), Oct CPI (mid-Nov), Dec FOMC, 10y vs 5.25 %, HY OAS vs 4 %, CRWV credit, midterms 11-03.
