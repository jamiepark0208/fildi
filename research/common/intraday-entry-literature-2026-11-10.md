## What this changes in the plan
1. **Two opposite forces, both documented:** single-stock reversal (Nagel, HKS, EOD reversal, EPM) vs market-level momentum driven by short gamma (Gao et al., Baltussen 2021). A sold put on a high-beta name in a crash tape is exposed to both → model must have the **index leg** (SPY first-30-min return, dealer gamma sign) and the **idiosyncratic leg** (stock vs SPY same-minute) as separate features. This is the "not a single if-then" structure Jamie asked for.
2. **When in the day:** literature points to the late session (after the market-momentum push, before or during the single-stock end-of-day pop), plus the option-side overnight edge (Muravyev-Ni). Pre-register candidate windows 10:30–11:30, 13:00–14:30, 15:00–15:30 rather than 'any minute after 60'.
3. **VIX enters twice:** higher P(reversion) (Nagel) and a bigger tail (crash = the reversal premium's risk) → output size, not only P.
4. **S186 winner pass agrees:** losers in sector_drop were sold after a bounce (sector +0.8 % 5d), winners into weakness (−1.0 %).
