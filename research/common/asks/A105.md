## What to survey (by category, with license checks)
1. **Forecasting**: time-series foundation models (open-source candle/forecasting models with
   uncertainty estimates). Fit: earnings-move prediction, regime features.
2. **Portfolio optimization**: riskfolio-lib / PyPortfolioOpt (risk parity, stress tests, factor
   models). Fit: fildi has no portfolio-level optimizer — put-selling book sizing is currently
   ad hoc; evaluate risk-parity / Kelly-flavored sizing for the short-premium book.
3. **Backtesting engines**: vectorbt, backtesting.py (Python); nautilus_trader, barter-rs
   (Rust, high-performance). Fit: A83 found 20 copy-paste backfill scripts and no shared
   harness — a real backtesting engine is the fix, not another script.
4. **Execution/research infra**: whatever the survey turns up for options-specific tooling.

## What to build (phased)
- **Phase 1 — survey**: for each category, 2–3 leading repos with stars, license, maturity,
  and a one-line fildi fit note. License-check everything (copyleft flags before adoption).
- **Phase 2 — gap map**: which fildi gaps each candidate fills (backtest harness ← A83,
  position sizing ← no current optimizer, forecasting ← earnings/IV work).
- **Phase 3 — pilot one**: the highest-leverage adoption (likely the backtest harness or
  risk-parity sizing) as a follow-up build plan with acceptance criteria.
