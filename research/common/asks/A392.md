## Spec
1. **scripts/watchlist_sync/put_manage.py** — for each open short put in the latest snapshot (existing loop), add `rolls`: buy back at ask (+ 23 % of spread, same fill rule as `captured`), sell candidates from the live chain (`http://127.0.0.1:8080/api/schwab/chain-ladder`, same call as `s172_paper.chain()`, expiries 5–45 DTE past the current one, strikes ≤ current strike) at bid + 10 % of spread. Keep up to 4 candidates: (a) same strike, next 1–2 expiries (roll out), (b) lower strike for net credit ≥ 0 (roll down & out), (c) the strike nearest the engine's target band for that name (put_recs_daily `band_target`). Each: net credit/debit, new strike, new expiry, new break-even, P(ITM at expiry) and P(touch) ≈ 2 × P(ITM) from the chain delta / IV (Black-Scholes, no new model), extra days of risk, annualized net credit on strike. Plus `assign`: effective cost basis = strike − total credits collected (incl. prior rolls if visible in portfolio_orders), vs spot. Plus `close`: realized loss now. Deadline 60 s per chain call; cap 15 names per run (open puts first by loss).
2. Store in `watchlist_sync.put_manage_daily` payload (existing table) under `rolls` / `assign` / `close`; no new table.
3. **API** `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/put-manage.ts` — pass the new fields through (types in put-manage-format.ts + test).
4. **UI** `artifacts/stock-compare/src/components/live/put-manage-alerts.tsx` (or its row) — per open put an expandable "Options" row: compact GRID_* table Hold · Close · Roll out · Roll down & out · Assign, columns net $ · new strike/expiry · break-even · P(ITM) · P(touch) · ann. %. Method in DataTip tooltips, no long text (UI style rules). Highlight none — Fildi shows, Jamie decides.
5. Tests: put-manage-format.test.ts covers the new fields; a python smoke that prints one name's rolls.
