# A161 — Portfolio VaR for the put-selling book

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Source:** A132 teardown winner #1 (FinceptTerminal steal-list, scored 5/5 for put seller)

## Ask
Add a portfolio-level tail-risk number to fildi: "if the market drops X, the book loses $Y."
Fildi has per-trade risk badges (A136) but no answer to "how much do I lose if everything goes wrong at once."

## Detail
- **Metric:** 95% and 99% VaR on the options book, computed two ways: (a) parametric (delta-normal from position Greeks + underlying vol), (b) historical (replay the last 2y of underlying returns through current positions). Show both; flag when they disagree by > 30%.
- **Scenario:** "market −10%" — reprice every short put at underlying × 0.9 (use current IV, no vol expansion = conservative underestimate, label it as such).
- **Placement:** Book card (A76) — one line: "95% VaR $X · 99% VaR $Y · −10% scenario $Z". Not a new tab.
- **Data:** positions from `pm_book`, prices from `prices_historical`, Greeks from `lib/options.ts`. No new data source.
- **Use case:** position sizing — "VaR exceeds 15% of book → stop selling premium until it cools." Expected: VaR number updates daily, backtested against realized worst-days (should exceed realized loss ~95% of days). Pass: on 2024–2026 history, 95% VaR breached on 4–7% of days (calibrated, not fantasy).
- **Out of scope:** Expected Shortfall, Monte Carlo with vol surface dynamics, intraday VaR. Keep it simple.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Book card shows 95%/99% VaR + −10% scenario, labeled with method and date.
- [ ] Historical backtest: breach rate 3–8% for 95% VaR over 2y (in `research/studies/`).
- [ ] Disagreement flag when parametric vs historical differ > 30%.
