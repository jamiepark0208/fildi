## Ask (Jamie 11-04, verbatim gist)
Signals are probabilities, not trades. Take risk in a risk-mitigated way. If X after a signal loses → test the opposite and test X *before* the signal. If neither up nor down works → test selling range (short straddle / iron condor / far OTM puts+calls). Don't judge one strategy at a fixed interval: a trader runs several structures, pivots by regime, and chooses holding period (not only intraday). Example: deep OTM short put on HOOD in the morning, stock rallies → theta + delta + IV drop pay.

## Detail
**States (per ticker, known at entry, no look-ahead):** (1) post-signal 0–3 bars, per indicator × side (+ S153 filters: first hour, 5m+15m agree, SPY+QQQ same side, squeeze-on); (2) pre-signal = quiet: no signal from any of the 6 indicators in the last N bars (N = 6/12/24 on 5m) and squeeze-off; (3) trend state: last flip side of ST/UT on 1h and daily; (4) vol state: IV rank and VRP = ATM IV − RV20 (option_chain_history; daily IV history from WRDS OptionMetrics / LSEG per `.agents/skills/fildi-market-data` for > 19 days).
**Structure menu, BOTH sides each:** long/short stock; long call/put; short put / short call (deep OTM, delta 10–20); debit + credit verticals; long + short straddle/strangle; iron condor. For every losing cell, its mirror (opposite side / long↔short premium) is reported next to it, not dropped.
**Holds:** intraday (30 m, 60 m, to close), multi-day (1, 3, 5 d), swing (to next opposite 1h/daily flip, capped 20 d); option DTE matched to hold (0–2 DTE intraday, 7–14 DTE multi-day, 30–45 DTE swing). Exit rules also tested: hold-to-expiry vs take-profit 50 % of credit / 100 % of debit, stop 2× credit, time stop 21 DTE (standard premium-selling rules; cite sources found via `scripts/fetch-page.sh`).
**Pricing:** chain at entry (fetched_at ≤ entry), RH fill bid + 23 % spread (ask − 23 % for sells), BS exit with entry IV ± 10 % sensitivity; multi-day uses that day's snapshot when present.
**Selection = walk-forward, not judgment:** pick the best structure per state on the first half by risk-adjusted return (Sortino; also report CVaR 5 %, max DD, win %, avg P&L % of risk); score it on the second half untouched. Baselines on the same dates: always-sell 30-delta put (PUT-index style), buy-and-hold, T-bill, vol-matched random entry.
**Pass (pre-registered):** a state → structure rule "works" if out-of-sample Sortino > the always-sell-put baseline AND > random, n ≥ 100 trades on ≥ 10 days, Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 on P&L vs random. Otherwise report MDE + data needed. One pass; no re-tuning after seeing the second half.
