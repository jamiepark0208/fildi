## Ask (Jamie 11-04, verbatim gist)
Signals are probabilities, not trades. Take risk in a risk-mitigated way. If X after a signal loses → test the opposite and test X *before* the signal. If neither up nor down works → test selling range (short straddle / iron condor / far OTM puts+calls). Don't judge one strategy at a fixed interval: a trader runs several structures, pivots by regime, and chooses holding period (not only intraday). Example: deep OTM short put on HOOD in the morning, stock rallies → theta + delta + IV drop pay.
