**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor

**Severity:** medium

## Context
Jamie asked (Oct 14): a plan to track the world's flow of money — major players and guesstimates,
entities like countries, richest individuals, companies, sectors — with modern flow-chart visuals
(entity orbs, Sankey), and public git repos to copy. Extensive web research (data sources, repos,
viz stack) + macro-tab inventory completed Oct 15.

## Research headline
**Data (free, personal-usable):**
- BIS Locational Banking Statistics — quarterly cross-border bank flows; free SDMX API, no key.
- IMF CPIS — bilateral portfolio investment (bond/equity), semiannual; free SDMX API / DBnomics.
- IMF DOTS — bilateral trade flows, monthly; free API.
- US Treasury TIC — monthly foreign holders of US securities; free archives.
- Fed Z.1 — quarterly US sectoral flows; free bulk CSV.
- World Bank WDI — market cap by country, remittances; free API.
- komed3/rtb-api — MIT-licensed Forbes billionaire mirror (~3,000 profiles) via free CDN JSON.
- Crypto: DeFiLlama (free), Etherscan (free key), Dune community SQL.
- ICI weekly mutual-fund flows (free); NBIM full holdings (free — best sovereign-wealth entity data).
- Institutional-only (flagged, not planned): SWIFT raw, EPFR, Crunchbase/PitchBook, paid Glassnode.

**Repos to copy:**
- lastch1ld/debtrank-globe — 3D globe + real BIS/CPIS pipeline, React Three Fiber (verify license;
  its data pipeline is the most reusable asset).
- trojansf/market-cockpit-pro — FRED-based Money Flow Sankey page.
- PrafullRaj/Crypto-Bubble — orb aesthetics reference.
- garadieu-create/CashFlow360, ynnckth/monny2sankey — Sankey patterns.

**Viz stack (React/TS):** Apache ECharts (Sankey/chord, canvas, thousands of links) · reagraph
(WebGL entity-orb networks) · flowmap.gl + deck.gl (animated geographic flows) · nivo (pretty
defaults, <200 nodes). Precompute normalized edge list {source,target,value,layer,period}.

**Honesty rule:** the page must separate flows vs positions vs ownership vs proxies, and label
guesstimates as estimates — extends the existing `LiquidityFlowDiagram` honesty convention.

## What to build (phased)
- **Phase 1 — entity registry**: countries, billionaires (rtb-api), SWFs (NBIM + rankings),
  mega-cap companies; static curated tables with source labels.
- **Phase 2 — flow edges**: BIS LBS + IMF CPIS + TIC pipelines → normalized edge list; Sankey view
  (country→country, sector→sector groupings).
- **Phase 3 — orb network**: reagraph entity graph — orbs sized by wealth/AUM, edges = flows;
  group by country/sector/entity-type.
- **Phase 4 — geo flows**: flowmap.gl animated particle map for cross-border flows.
- **Phase 5 — scheduled refresh**: weekly edge rebuild; staleness badges (CPIS lags ~1yr).
