# A94 — Conviction ledger: structured investment theses with evidence, updating, kill criteria

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Severity:** medium-high (this is the "why am I in this trade" system)

## Context
Jamie asked (Oct 14), thinking aloud: is there a framework for building "conviction" — the human
sense behind a buy like HOOD (Trump accounts, prediction markets, AI agentic features, PDT rule
removal, country expansion)? Verdict: not bogus. The valuable core is a structured thesis ledger
that turns unlimited scattered inputs (earnings calls, financials, analyst views, sentiment, macro,
government policy) into tracked, revisable conviction. It unifies A80 (short-horizon thesis scoring)
and A85 (external views) — those become inputs, not duplicates.

## The HOOD example, structured
His HOOD buy wasn't one conviction — it was five sub-theses: (1) Trump accounts → new accounts/
deposits, (2) prediction markets → new revenue line, (3) AI agentic features → engagement, (4) PDT
rule removal → trading revenue, (5) country expansion → TAM. Each has its own evidence,
invalidation, and timeline. The ledger models exactly this: a thesis is a bundle of falsifiable
sub-theses, each with "what would prove me wrong."

## What to build
1. **Thesis object**: {ticker, claim, horizon, confidence, sub_theses[], evidence[] (linked to
   fildi data: earnings transcripts, financials, analyst views, sentiment, macro series, A85 figure
   views), key_risks[], invalidation[] ("what makes me exit"), position_link}.
2. **Evidence linking**: every sub-thesis links to the fildi data that supports it — not prose, but
   pointers (transcript quote + date, metric + value, analyst action + firm). New evidence appends;
   history is never rewritten.
3. **Conviction updating**: confidence moves when evidence arrives (up/down with reason required);
   the ledger shows the conviction history as a sparkline — "why did I believe this more in March?"
   stays answerable.
4. **Kill criteria / pre-mortem**: mandatory invalidation field per sub-thesis. The most valuable
   part for a put seller: the exit is pre-decided, not improvised under stress.
5. **Scheduled review**: weekly open-thesis review — prompt to update, reaffirm, or kill each one;
   dead theses (invalidation hit) auto-flag. Ties to A80's calibration loop and A92's
   assignment-aware P&L as the outcome measure.

## What's deliberately NOT in this plan
- No black-box "conviction score" from an LLM — confidence is Jamie's, the ledger just tracks it
  and its history honestly.
- No auto-trading on thesis changes — display and review only, per the no-AI-trading rule.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Thesis CRUD with sub-theses, evidence links, invalidation criteria.
- [ ] Conviction history visible per thesis; updates require a reason.
- [ ] Weekly review surfaces open theses needing attention; invalidated theses flagged.
- [ ] A80 and A85 feed in as evidence/short-horizon calibration, not separate silos.
