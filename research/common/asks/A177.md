# A177: [quant] 0DTE tail-lotto: do we have real intraday option prices to backtest it?
**From → to:** Claude → Claude · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Status:** accepted (data pass with caveats → backtest next)
**Moves:** put/call pnl (a new long-option lane, if the data exists)

## Ask
Jamie's friend traded 0DTE options: after an extreme market move, buy very far OTM same-day options and occasionally hit a huge winner (18000 %). The idea makes sense: on a panic day, far-OTM 0DTE options may be priced for a move the market doesn't expect. Before designing the backtest, check whether we have honest prices for it.

## Detail
- **Why a model won't do:** S021 found our model option prices ran about 25 % too cheap OTM, and far-OTM 0DTE pricing is the worst case (all skew and convexity). A model-priced lotto backtest would show fiction → **not allowed**.
- **Probe** (one capped command per source, run before calling anything "down"):
  1. Polygon: does our plan return minute aggregates or quotes for SPY/QQQ 0DTE contracts in 2026? Check 3 sample days, including one big-move day.
  2. Schwab: does the chain history hold any same-day expiries intraday?
  3. Cost of an upgrade if neither has it (free-first; state the $).
- **Pass:** real intraday option prices for at least 6 months of 2026 on SPY or QQQ, at least every 5 minutes, covering far-OTM strikes (Δ ≤ 0.05) → file A177b, a pre-registered study. Draft it now:
  - Trigger: SPY intraday move ≥ k·σ (k fixed before the test) or VIX jump.
  - Entry: the next bar's ask.
  - Exit: the close or a 10× target.
  - Outcome: pnl per $ risked, every trigger day included (no survivorship).
  - Report: hit rate, the size of the largest win, and how much of the pnl it carries.
- **Fail:** park it with the decline check:
  - Need: catch mispriced tails on extreme days.
  - Met by / fails because: no real intraday prices.
  - Revisit trigger: an intraday options feed added, or 6 months of our own 0DTE snapshots collected.
  - Option: start collecting SPY 0DTE snapshots now if that's cheap.
- **Caveat for Jamie:** 2026 alone may hold only 0–3 true tail days. Even a pass is thin evidence, and the page has to say so.
