## Findings (High)

- **H1 — Session-local RSI cold start.** `scripts/watchlist_sync/analysis/conditional_engine.py`, `rsi_series()` / `entry_cross_mask()`: RSI is computed per session with an EWM from bar 1, no warm-up and no `min_periods`. Verified: RSI = 0.0 at bar 1 on a down-open, ~93 at bar 3 on tiny mixed moves. The flagship RSI<10 spec fires on nearly every down-open day — trade count, win rate and lift are all dominated by fake oversold entries. Fix: warm up from prior sessions' closes, or require `min_periods` and NaN-guard the first N bars; re-run smoke + universe and report the trade-count change.
- **H2 — IV-beta sign inverted.** `load_iv_return_betas()` returns β = Cov(lr, ΔlogIV)/Var(lr) (typically negative: IV rises when spot falls), but `iv_intraday_path()` uses `exp(-beta * lr)` — IV *falls* on down moves instead of rising. Short-put marks are understated on losers (SLs trigger late or never); every PnL / win-rate is biased in favour of short puts. Fix: `exp(beta * lr)`. Muse verified from the diff.
- **H3 — Carried positions reuse stale session-local `fill_bar`.** `simulate_session_puts()` L459–476 indexes the next session with the prior session's bar index; if the prior fill was bar 60, no exit is evaluated until ~bar 61 (~14:35) — gap-down opens and morning SLs are missed. Corrupts `tp50_overnight` / `hold_expiry` variants only.

## Findings (Medium — fix in the same pass)

- **M1** Exits fill at the signal bar's close (the same close that triggered the condition) while entries correctly wait for the next bar's open — inflates TP capture; inconsistent with the file's own convention.
- **M2** TP/SL evaluated only on 5m closes; `h`/`l` are loaded but never used — intrabar excursions through SL/TP are invisible, no gap-through handling.
- **M3** Headline PnL uses `net_mid` (half round-trip spread) instead of `net_cross` — overstates mean PnL ~20bp of collateral per trade at rt=0.40.
- **M4** `n_eff` formula wrong: `sum(1/count per cluster key)` — 10 trades in one cluster reports 0.1 instead of 1. Frontier `N_eff` column wrong wherever clustering occurs.
- **M5** Fresh entry on the penultimate bar books a fake breakeven trade; last-bar signals are silently dropped.
- **M6** Base benchmark is skipped on carry sessions and there is no re-entry after a carry closes — the base sample is conditioned on the conditional strategy not being in a trade, biasing lift.
- **Tests** (`a359_conditional_test.py`, 7 tests, 3 genuinely assertive): `test_one_fire_per_edge` is vacuous (asserts only strictly-increasing indices, true for any mask — never asserts `len(idx)==1`); `test_simulation_uses_iv_anchor_not_session_eod` is tautological (echoes its own input; the simulation takes no EOD IV input, so it cannot fail); no test exercises the close-condition exit (RSI>50) — the heart of A359 — and the engine silently never fires non-rsi14 close conditions. `o==c` fixtures can't catch fill-price errors.
- **Catalog / definition drift:** `indicator_catalog.py` advertises 18 indicators the engine cannot compute (`condition_true_series` raises `NotImplementedError` except rsi2/5/14; exits only handle rsi14) — implement or trim the catalog. The daily grid and the engine use different RSI definitions (grid: `min_periods=n`, zero avg-down → 100; engine: from bar 1, → NaN) — canonicalize one definition or the "same" RSI condition is not comparable across A359 and S159.
