## Detail
- **H1 — RSI slice misaligned under `session_filter`.** `ctx.rsi_by_ticker` is computed on the unfiltered `bars_map`, but the frame is filtered (e.g. OPRA window) and re-indexed before slicing `rsi_full[i0:i1]` (`_run_ticker_sessions` ~L803, `run_conditional_strategy` ~L922). Every OPRA-window sweep/survivor row reads RSI from the wrong dates. Evidence: the commit's own smoke gate works around this by recomputing RSI after trimming `bars_map`; the `session_filter` path does not. Fix: compute RSI after filtering, or slice by timestamp join; re-run OPRA-window sweeps and report the number change. Pass: OPRA-window and unfiltered runs agree on RSI dates on a hand-checked ticker.
- **H2 — Lookahead leak on carried positions' bar 0.** `_exit_fill_from_signal` (~L475) + `simulate_session_puts` (~L567): a carried position's bar-0 TP/SL/close trigger is computed from bar 0's full high/low/close but filled at bar 0's open. `TestCarriedStopBarZero` encodes it (gap to open 70, low 69 triggers the stop, fill at open 70). Pure `time_stop` at bar 0 is causal; price-triggered bar-0 exits are not. Note: this bar-0 open fill was the fix for d55dec7's one-bar-late overnight exit — the fix trades lateness for lookahead. Fix: evaluate bar-0 exits on prior-session close / bar-0 open only, or fill at bar-0 close; update the test to assert the fill price is consistent with the trigger. Pass: carried bar-0 exits never use bar-0 high/low/close.
- **M1 — `chain_put_mid` fallback has no distance cap (a605c2c, ~L950).** First query requires `abs(strike − target) < 0.51`; the fallback picks the nearest strike by `ORDER BY abs(...) LIMIT 1` with no cap, so an illiquid/missing expiry silently prices the contract at a distant strike's mid. Fix: cap the fallback distance (same 0.51, or 1.0) and return no-quote when exceeded. Pass: a missing-expiry case returns no-quote instead of a distant strike's mid.
