## Context
From @fidetolabs' reel ("How To Build A Self-Improving AI Trading Agent #1"). No tool —
a methodology warning, and it lands hard on fildi: their dataset of 5,908 news
articles (37% published after market close) showed Sharpe **3.18** when articles were
filed under their printed date vs **0.98** when filed under the first day you could
actually trade on them. Data leakage looks like alpha; a self-improving loop will
hunt for whichever variation leaks hardest.

## Why it matters now
A120's pattern-discovery backtests are running (SPY reversal, Asia lead-lag, etc.),
A51's event-window study is pre-registered, and A56/A57 all backtest. If any of them
file events/news under printed dates instead of first-tradable timestamps, the
"discoveries" are leakage artifacts. This audit is a gate on trusting those results.

## Proposal (Cursor audits; Claude [quant] reviews the findings)
1. Inventory every backtest/event-study code path: `backtest_puts.py`, A120 research
   scripts, A51 event-study harness, A56/A57 — list how each timestamps news/events.
2. For each: classify as **clean** (first-tradable-time alignment), **leaky** (printed
   date / close-price peeking), or **unknown**.
3. Fix leaky paths: shift event timestamps to first tradable open (or next session

4. Re-run the flagship A120 findings (SPY reversal, Asia lead-lag) under the fixed
   convention; report which results survive.
5. Add a standing rule: every new backtest declares its timestamp convention up front
   (extends the A47/A51/A52/A56 pre-registration discipline).
