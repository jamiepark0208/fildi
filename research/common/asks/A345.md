## 1. Problem (repo-watch finding, 2026-11-05 evening run)

`GET /stocks/competitors/:ticker` fills the peers box with same-sector names by market cap when the taxonomy group has fewer than 16 names — and the code comment says "Scoring cohort stays the taxonomy group." It does not: the extended `peers` list is passed straight into `rankCompetitors`, which (a) ranks the subject over the whole passed universe (`combinedScore` sort, rank 1..25), and (b) percentile-ranks fallback fund scores within the passed rows (`computeFundScoresForRows` ranks each metric over `fundRows`, which are fetched for the extended `allTickers`). So for any competitor missing a persisted fund score, its fallback fund score — and its combined score and rank — now depend on the sector-fill names, and the subject's rank is measured against a mixed cohort. The `inGroup` flag marks fill names in the response, but the ranks themselves are computed over the mixed set.

Severity: low. Display ranking only; persisted fund scores (the common path) are unaffected. But the comment promises an invariant the code breaks, and ranks are user-visible.

## 2. Evidence (branch cursor/mac-dev @ caa1c44)

- `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/stocks.ts`, competitors route: `if (peers.length < 16)` appends sector names to `peers`; then `rankCompetitors(key, peers, techRows, fundRows, ...)` with the extended list, and `fundRows` are fetched for `allTickers = [key, ...peers]` (extended).
- `artifacts/api-server/src/lib/peer-rankings.ts`, `rankCompetitors`: `universe = [key, ...peerTickers]`; sorts the whole cohort by `combinedScore` and assigns `rank: i + 1`.
- `artifacts/api-server/src/lib/peer-rankings.ts`, `computeFundScoresForRows`: percentile-ranks each fund metric over the passed `rows` — the cohort defines the score.
- The route comment ("Scoring cohort stays the taxonomy group. Fill the peers box …") sits directly above the fill block.

## 3. Fix direction

Pick one and make the comment true: (a) rank only the taxonomy group (pass the pre-fill `peers` to `rankCompetitors` and fetch `techRows`/`fundRows` for that set), then append fill names to the response afterwards as display-only rows with `inGroup: false` and no rank; or (b) keep the mixed ranking and correct the comment + surface the mixed cohort honestly (fill names ranked alongside, labelled). (a) is preferred — it preserves the existing rank semantics and keeps the fill purely cosmetic.

## 4. Pass / fail

- Competitors response for a ticker with a small taxonomy group: ranks 1..N cover only taxonomy-group names; sector-fill names (if shown) carry `inGroup: false` and no rank, or are absent from the ranked list.
- A competitor missing a persisted fund score gets the same fallback fund score with and without the sector fill (same taxonomy cohort in both cases).
- Existing competitors tests / CI green.
