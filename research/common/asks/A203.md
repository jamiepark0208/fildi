**From → to:** Muse → Claude · **Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** open

**Correction (Jamie, 2026-10-20):** Muse's first verdict dismissed the corpus as data-mined. Wrong instinct — with 100k candidates the job is to build the selection funnel and let fildi's validation do the killing. A haystack is a source, not a conclusion.

## Step 1 — access due diligence (before anything else)
- Identify Jev precisely: product, vendor, price/access model. Can strategies be browsed/filtered/exported (rules + stated stats), or is it a closed app with screenshots only?
- If the corpus isn't accessible in structured form → kill at step 1 with the reason recorded. No scraping heroics.

## Step 2 — the funnel (pre-registered filters, in order)
1. Market: US equities/indices only (drop forex/crypto/futures-only).
2. Timeframe: daily or slower (matches the put book's decision cadence).
3. Stated stats: creator-side out-of-sample or walk-forward numbers present; in-sample-only → drop.
4. Simplicity: ≤5 parameters (each extra parameter is an overfit coupon).
5. Survivors → re-implement the top 5 in fildi's harness on fildi data → A131 checklist + DSR/PBO discipline. Expected yield honestly: 0–2 strategies worth a second look. That is a fine outcome for the cost.

## Second use of the corpus (even if every strategy dies)
A large set of known-overfit strategies is a **calibration set for the validator itself**: run fildi's DSR/PBO gauntlet on strategies designed to look good in-sample. If the gauntlet passes obvious junk, the gauntlet is broken — cheaper to learn that here than on a strategy we believe in.

## Downstream
- Any survivor → its own [quant] study row before any build (A51 pattern). Nothing from Jev touches the book directly, ever.
- Funnel code (filters over strategy metadata) is reusable for future corpora (e.g., the 2/151 series, A204).

## Use cases (Muse, in priority order)
1. **Mined strategy candidates.** 100k strategies → 5 pre-registered filters → ~5 re-implementations → expected yield 0–2 strategies earning their own [quant] study. Benefit if even one survives: a tested strategy we did not have to invent, for the cost of filtering.
2. **Validator calibration set.** Known-overfit strategies are test dummies for fildi's DSR/PBO gauntlet: if the gauntlet passes junk, we learn the gauntlet is broken here — cheap — instead of on a strategy we believe in.
3. **Reusable funnel.** The filter pipeline (market → timeframe → OOS-evidence → simplicity) applies to any future strategy corpus (creator series, A204's 151, papers). Build once, reuse.
