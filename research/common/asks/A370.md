## Ask
Every intraday indicator was killed on "does direction pay after spread". Jamie sells puts, so the right test is "when the indicator fires, is premium richer than the move that follows, and is premium kept higher?", run **inside the universe where the trade is placed** (Jamie 11-07: sell where IV is highest, buy where IV is lowest). Claude's smoke test says yes for a few daily filters; scale it and pre-register.

## Detail
**Smoke (Claude 11-07, `scripts/watchlist_sync/analysis/a370_put_timing_smoke.py`, `a370_regime_split.py`, `a370_by_year.py`; ~5 s, cache only).** IV panel really starts 2020 (≈190–250 names to 2024, 677 in 2025, 911 in 2026). Weekly sample, top-IV tercile by cross-sectional iv30_put rank, seller edge = iv30 − realised vol next 5 d, vs same-week others:
- SuperTrend(10,3) bear +1.8 vol pts t 7.8 (both halves); below 200d +2.5 t 8.9 (pre-2023 t 1.7); 5-d drop ≤ −5 % +1.9 t 4.3; squeeze +1.1 t 2.5; RSI > 70 −4.4 t −4.9; relvol ≥ 1.5 −4.4 t −6.0. One-bar shift leak check: unchanged.
- Large-cap-only run (10 names) gave the opposite for drops / RSI < 30 (−7 vol pts): universe matters.
- **"2023 break" is regime + N, not date:** below-200d works when SPY > 200d (+3.5, t 9.0) and fades when SPY < 200d (+0.8, t 2.4); 5-d drop +3.8 (t 6.0) when < 40 % of names are below their 200d, +0.2 when ≥ 40 %. 2020 and 2022 were broad sell-offs; 2023–26 is mostly idiosyncratic dips in an up market, plus 3× the rows.
- Real LSEG puts (3,118 top-IV vs 4,682 rest): top-IV median +53 bp/wk vs +24 (strikes 11 % vs 6 % OTM) but mean +4.5 vs +20: a few −20 to −25 %-of-strike weeks erase it. No filter t ≥ 2 in both halves on real quotes (tails, mixed strikes).
- Buy side (bottom-IV tercile): no edge. Long puts −48 % on premium; RV < IV even in cheap names (−2.5 vol pts). Calls in cache: 5 low-IV names only.

**Full run (pre-register before running; write the bar into INDEX first):**
1. Universe: each week, top-IV tercile (sell) and top IV/RV tercile, all names with iv30_put. Comparator = same week, same tercile, **matched delta bucket** (not mixed strikes), one trade per name-week.
2. Filters: the 8 daily ones in the smoke + the dead intraday set (UT Bot, SuperTrend, MSO CHoCH, VWAP, squeeze, chandelier) computed on 1h bars at the entry bar (A359 engine, LSE minute data), + A339 triple-RSI(5).
3. Outcomes: (a) iv − RV over the **option's own life** (fix the smoke's 30d-vs-5d mismatch), (b) premium kept on real LSEG quotes (bid + 23 % spread, hold to expiry), (c) breach rate and worst-5 % week.
4. Regime cut: SPY above/below 200d and breadth (share of names below 200d) reported for every cell; VIX level once VIX is in the panel.
5. Tail guard variants: no earnings inside expiry; skip names with a 5-d drop > 15 %.
6. **Pass:** same-week, matched-delta lift t ≥ 2 in both halves (split 2023-01) after Holm across all cells, AND worst-5 % week no worse than comparator. Stop rule: if no cell passes on (a), skip (b) for the intraday set.
7. Buy side: needs LSEG call quotes for the bottom-IV tercile (budget check first, `fildi-market-data`); park unless (a) shows RV > IV for some filter.
