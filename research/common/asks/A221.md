## 2nd-level analysis
Adaptation in four pieces, all from data fildi already stores (Schwab+Yahoo merged daily bars):
1. Covariance of underlyings (trailing window, shrinkage estimator), delta-adjusted exposures → effective number of bets + correlation clusters (rho > 0.7).
2. Two-regime covariance: calm Sigma for hygiene; stress Sigma from the worst ~5% of market days for tail honesty (correlations run to 1 in selloffs).
3. Marginal risk per new put at ENTRY: book stress-loss with vs without the candidate, surfaced beside the A213 odds panel. This is also the Portfolio-Optimization / Risk-Parity gap from the 20-strategy map (premium per unit marginal risk; equal tail-risk contribution sizing).
4. Convexity rule: Sigma is used for STRUCTURE (clusters, ranking, caps); tail NUMBERS come from factor-model scenarios (market + cluster + idio drawn from stress Sigma) with full option repricing. Delta-linear P&L understates a short-gamma book's tails.
The flat -10% scenario is NOT replaced — it stays as the auditable floor. Covariance sits above it.

## Study design (pre-registered)
Replay S003 history (~17k puts, 2020-04→): rebuild the day-by-day book; at each entry (trailing data only) compute cluster + post-add cluster share of stress-VaR; skip entries that breach the 35% cap; recompute the book's daily P&L path.
- Pass: worst-5-days aggregate loss falls >=25%; max drawdown falls >=15%; premium kept >=90% of actual; assignments do not increase; blocked entries <=30% (above that the cap is just trading less).
- Regime split reported separately: 2020, 2022 bear, calm years. A cap that only helps in 2022 is a different product.
- Fail: gauge ships display-only (use case 1), no trading-rule claims.
