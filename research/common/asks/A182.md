# A182: Model-version promotion protocol — scoreboard-gated, preregistered

**From → to:** Muse → Cursor · **Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Status:** open
**Severity:** high (a bad promotion touches the live book)

## Thesis

S046 just re-tested the call lane into options-1.2.0 (−volume −200d stretch,
**+42.9 bp/wk OOS** on covered-call pnl — fcac601, 2026-10-19). A172 (planned) will
grade 1.0.0 vs 1.1.0 live in shadow mode. A167 says grade at ≥ 8 expiries. The
missing piece is the decision rule: under what preregistered conditions does a
shadow champion get promoted to the live book? Without it, the shop either promotes
on vibes (live-book damage) or sits on real edge for months (the +42.9 bp/wk case
right now). This row writes the rule once; it then judges every future version.

## Why now

- options-1.2.0 is the first test case sitting on the desk today — the protocol has
  an immediate verdict to render (promote or hold, with a dated reason).
- A172's shadow infra is being planned now; the promotion rule should be
  preregistered (A131) *before* the shadow numbers start arriving, not fit to them
  afterward.
- A131's 4-step validation checklist (parameter stability, Monte Carlo, cluster
  analysis, walk-forward) gives the technical spine; this row is the governance
  wrapper.

## What changes (plan-level, no code)

1. **Protocol doc** (this file's Status notes or linked .md): promotion requires ALL of:
   (a) ≥ 8 expiries graded live-or-shadow vs the incumbent (A167's bar);
   (b) edge ≥ +X bp/wk vs incumbent on the A171 scoreboard (thresholds Claude-set,
   per lane — puts vs calls differ);
   (c) no S015-style left-tail violation (worst-5-week loss not worse than
   incumbent's at 95% confidence — the A136-framed test, not "filters losers");
   (d) Jamie's final tap (A17: promotions stay Jamie).
2. **Demotion symmetric:** incumbent losing by the same margins for 8 expiries gets
   a demotion proposal — no sacred versions.
3. **First application:** judge options-1.2.0 against the rule on the S046 evidence;
   verdict = promote / hold-with-reason / needs-more-expiries, dated.
4. **Ledger:** every verdict lands in OUTCOMES.md (AGENT_OPS.md §1) — the outcome
   audit then grades whether promotions actually helped.

## Use cases

- UC1: options-1.2.0 shows +42.9 bp/wk OOS — protocol says whether 1 study is
  enough (it isn't: needs 8 expiries) and what the hold reason is.
- UC2: A172 shadow shows 1.1.0 beating 1.0.0 for 8 expiries on put Brier —
  promotion is automatic-proposal, not a debate.

## Expected results

Quantitative: edge claims get a falsifiable bar (e.g. "+42.9 bp/wk holds at ≥ +20
bp/wk over 8 live expiries or it's demoted"). Qualitative: promotion debates end;
the ledger shows whether promoted versions earned it.

## Risks

- Thresholds need Claude's quant judgment — that IS the approval gate, not a risk
  to route around.
- 8 expiries is slow for fast-moving regimes — the protocol allows an emergency
  Jamie override (logged, per A136's override pattern), never a silent one.
- Overlaps A172's plan — this row is the decision rule, A172 is the measurement
  infra. Keep them separate; the rule must not be fit to the numbers.
