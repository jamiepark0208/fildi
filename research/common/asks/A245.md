## Method (pre-register in INDEX before running)
1. **Label extrema, no look-ahead in features:** swing low = lowest close within ±20 sessions AND the stock then rises ≥ 1.5 × its 20-d σ·√20 within 20 sessions; swing high mirrored. Panel: lseg_research.daily_history ~265 names 2018-10→ (+ prices_historical), Polygon option closes 2024-10→ for option features.
2. **Features at t (known that day only):** drawdown from 52w high, 5/20-d return z, RSI(14)/(2), MFI, distance to 50/200-d, volume spike (vs 60-d), gap count, IV30, IV rank, IV/RV20, IV change 5 d, call−put IV spread + put skew (Polygon window), short interest if present, SPY regime / breadth (recompute from the panel pre-2025), earnings within ±5 d, headline/news count if a stored source exists.
3. **Compare** extrema days vs (a) all days and (b) *look-alike* days — same drawdown/return bucket that did NOT become an extremum (the real question: what separates a crash that bottoms from one that keeps falling). Logistic / gradient-boosted classifier + per-feature univariate tests; Holm over features.
4. **Holdout:** fit 2018-10→2023-12, test 2024-01→ untouched (A244's 2025-07 holdout already seen). Pass = holdout AUC ≥ 0.60 vs look-alikes AND the top-decile signal's forward 20-d stock return beats the look-alike decile, block t ≥ 2. Prior to beat: S025 (no bounce edge after 2σ drops; SPY<200d recovery the only Holm pass), S051 (nothing separates laggard winners), S079 (downside AUC 0.52).
5. **Output:** the frozen rule's flags today (incl. RKLB, CRWV), packet ≤ 20 lines in § Status notes, one hand-checkable event.
Smoke test 5 names + hand-check 3 labeled extrema first.
