# A350 — [quant] IV Walls reversal rule: minute-bar test on fildi IV walls

**Status:** planned
Filed 2026-11-06 by Muse → Claude → Cursor. Source: Instagram reel DfVLyoQBb4w (@austingreen, Nov 4 2026) — "IV Walls putting me at the top." Tregify tournament, 3rd place, $9,000 on a 450-point move.

## 1st-level analysis (Muse)

The reel's rule: a quant software computes the expected daily range from historical vol + contract pricing ("IV walls"); 90% chance price stays inside; when price tests a wall, trade the reversal, stops below. For a put seller this is the same object as the expected move — the range within which premium is collected — so if the reversal edge is real, it is a timing signal for put entries, not just a day-trade trick.

**Muse's VM first pass (Nov 6, SPY daily 1993–2026, 8,500 bars, script `~/workspace/tmp/ivwalls_test.py`):** walls proxied by 20d realized vol at z=1.645 (no IV history on the VM). Results:

- The 90% claim FAILS on the proxy: only **78.8%** of days stay inside the walls (touch-up 10.0% of days, touch-down 11.4%).
- Wall-touch days are **trend days, not reversal days**: after touching the upper wall, P(close<open) is **12%** vs 47% base, mean intraday **+0.89%**; after touching the lower wall, P(close>open) is **18%** vs 52% base, mean intraday **−0.90%**. The stated rule is the opposite of what daily bars show.
- Next-day: no edge (touch-up +0.02%, touch-down +0.15% vs +0.05% base). 5d: touch-down +0.45% vs +0.23% all — weak.
- Median breach beyond the wall is small (0.23% up / 0.37% down).

**Caveat that keeps this alive:** the reel's version is intraday with real IV walls, and daily bars cannot see intraday reversals — a day can touch a wall, reverse, and still close near the extreme. The daily test kills the daily version of the rule; the intraday version is untested. This row tests the real claim, not the proxy.

## 2nd-level analysis

The honest prior is momentum, not mean reversion: the daily screen says walls get run through, not rejected. An intraday reversal edge, if it exists, lives in the first 30–60 minutes after the touch and dies fast — so the test must be on minute bars with real IV walls (fildi's S048 minute bars + the options score's IV data), with entry at the touch, a reversion target, and a stop beyond the wall. Costs matter: a reversal scalp that wins 55% at 1:1 is a loser after the spread on most names; the pass bar is expectancy after costs, not win rate. The rule also overlaps fildi's existing expected-move/IV-rank coverage — Step 1 checks that first so this row never duplicates a live field.

## Use cases (Claude: propose additional ones before running)

1. **Put-entry timing:** sell puts when price tests the lower IV wall and rejects it — the reversal becomes the entry, not the trade itself.
2. **Day-trade reversal signal:** the reel's literal rule, intraday, on the scored book's names — only if Step 2 clears its bar.
3. **Expected-move calibration:** the wall-hit rate itself (78.8% on the HV proxy) is a calibration check on fildi's expected-move field — if real IV walls also hold only ~80%, the field is overstating its confidence.

## Study design (pre-registered)

- Universe: SPY + the scored book's liquid names (same set as the A339 put-pnl harness where possible).
- Walls: from fildi IV (front-week ATM straddle / expected move), computed at the open; touch = high/low reaching the wall intraday on S048 minute bars.
- Rule: enter the reversal at the touch, target = mid-range (prior close), stop = 0.5× the wall distance beyond the wall. Measure win rate, expectancy after spread/slippage, max adverse excursion, and the share of touches that run through the wall.
- **Pass bar:** expectancy > 0 after costs AND beats the put-pnl harness timing baseline (A339) on the same names/dates; wall-hit rate within ±5pp of the stated 90% on real IV walls.
- **Stop rule:** if >60% of intraday touches run through the wall (same momentum signature as the daily screen), close as failed validation — the rule is a trend signal mislabeled as a reversal.
- **Step 1 (before any test):** check fildi's existing expected-move / IV-rank fields (options score, put engine) and record what already exists; this row adds only what is missing.
- **Step 2:** minute-bar test above, on S048 bars + fildi IV walls.
- **Step 3:** if Step 2 passes, wire as a timing signal shadow-first (S151 pattern: shadow put-climax signals, no live effect), then propose a put-entry filter; if it fails, close as failed validation with the numbers.
