## Detail (ranked: evidence × our data × cost)
1. **Options are rich intraday, cheap overnight** (Muravyev & Ni 2020, JFE: delta-hedged option returns are negative during the day and positive overnight).
   - Trade: sell SPY/QQQ options at the open, buy back at the close; or the reverse, hold overnight.
   - Data: LSEG daily bid/ask gives close-to-close; Polygon minute prints give open vs close.
2. **End-of-day hedging flows → last-30-min momentum** (Baltussen, Da, Lammers & Martens 2021, JFE). Dealers short gamma and leveraged ETFs (Cheng & Madhavan 2009) must buy into up-days and sell into down-days near the close.
   - Trade: from 15:30, go with the day's move once it's beyond ±1σ.
   - Data: SPY minute cache (2 years), plus LSEG OI for a dealer-gamma sign → a stronger version.
3. **0DTE straddle mispricing by regime.** FlashAlpha (193 SPY sessions, 2022–26): price closed outside the 10:00 1σ band on 25.9 % of days vs ~31.7 % fair → selling is slightly rich, but the losses sit on news days.
   - Test: sell defined-risk 0DTE flies on *non*-event, low RV/IV days, and buy them on S052 "volatile days". Both sides of one mispricing.
   - Data: Polygon minute prints + S052 score.
4. **Expiry pinning** (Ni, Pearson & Poteshman 2005, JFE): on expiration Fridays, stocks with large OI near a strike close at it more often than chance.
   - Trade: on opex, sell a tight fly at the max-OI strike, or fade moves away from it.
   - Data: **LSEG OI history per contract** (new 10-19) + daily closes.
5. **Liquidity-shock reversal, scaled by VIX** (Nagel 2012, RFS: short-term reversal = paid liquidity provision, larger when VIX is high).
   - Trade: fade a single stock's ≥ 3σ 30-min move with no news, held to the close. Size up when VIX is high.
   - Data: `intraday_bars_1m` (60 days) + WRDS TAQ 2026 for real spreads + `/v2/reference/news` to filter no-news moves.
6. **Pre-FOMC drift** (Lucca & Moench 2015, JF): SPY rises in the ~24 h before FOMC announcements.
   - Trade: long SPY the prior afternoon until 14:00 on announcement day, or a short-dated call.
   - Data: SPY minute (2 years ≈ 16 meetings) + macro-static dates. A small n, so it's a calendar overlay rather than a standalone strategy.
7. **Same-half-hour return persistence** (Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka 2010, JF): a stock's return in a given 30-min slot predicts the same slot on following days, from recurring institutional flows.
   - Data: `intraday_bars_1m` / TAQ.
8. **Earnings-gap continuation intraday:** big post-earnings gaps on high volume keep going through day 1 (PEAD shows up early).
   - Trade: enter at 10:00 in the gap direction, exit at the close.
   - Data: LSEG earnings dates + minute bars.
9. **Overnight vs intraday split** (Lou, Polk & Skouras 2019, JFE): momentum profits come overnight, reversal profits intraday.
   - Trade: the market-on-close → open leg for winners. Not strictly intraday, but it sets when to hold.
10. **Opex-week / Monday-after-opex flows:** vanna/charm unwinds after monthly expiry. Folk evidence only → low priority; test only after 1–5.

**Order:** 2 and 6 first (SPY minute data is cached, so it's free), then 1 and 3 (minute option prints), then 4 (LSEG OI), then 5/7/8 (need TAQ / news filters).
