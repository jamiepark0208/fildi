**Severity:** low

## Context
Jamie: "my ui needs much improvement." From @fifbuilds' reel (free GitHub animation
libraries). Verified 2026-10-16 — all four repos real:
- **darkroomengineering/lenis** (16.5K stars, MIT, active): buttery smooth scrolling.
- **DavidHDev/react-bits** (49K stars, active): drop-in React text/UI animations.
- greensock/GSAP (29K stars, active): full animation suite — heavier than needed here.
- tengbao/vanta (7.2K stars, MIT): 3D animated backgrounds — STALE (last push
  Mar 2024, unmaintained) and wrong vibe for a trading terminal. Skip.

## Proposal (Cursor builds)
Tasteful motion polish on the fildi frontend (stock-compare), not a redesign:
1. **Lenis**: smooth scrolling on long pages (scanner, macro tab). One wrapper, low risk.
2. **react-bits**: animated text/UI accents where they aid comprehension — e.g.,
   animated numbers on P&L/portfolio deltas, shimmer on loading states, staggered
   entrances on cards. NOT decoration for its own sake.
3. Rules: respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no animation on data-dense tables or
   charts (never slow down the numbers); keep bundle impact measured.

## Out of scope
- Vanta (stale + wrong for a terminal), full GSAP (overkill — react-bits covers it).
- No changes to chart rendering itself (that's A126).
