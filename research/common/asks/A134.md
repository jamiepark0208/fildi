# A134 — Gamma exposure (GEX) regime indicator for the put-selling book

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-16
**Severity:** high

## Context
From @nickelninjatrades' reel (Gamma Exposure University pitch). The course is a
product; the concept is public knowledge and directly relevant: options dealer
positioning drives intraday price behavior. In negative-gamma regimes, market-maker
hedging AMPLIFIES moves (buy strength/sell weakness); in positive-gamma regimes,
hedging dampens them. For a put seller, short premium into negative gamma is
picking up pennies in front of a steamroller.

## Why it matters
Jamie's book is short premium. A GEX regime readout tells him WHEN to sell (positive
gamma, dampened moves, premium decays quietly) and when to stand down or go further
OTM (negative gamma, amplified moves). This is portfolio protection, not alpha-chasing.

## Proposal (Cursor builds)
1. Compute net gamma exposure per strike from the existing Schwab option chains:
   GEX(strike) = Σ gamma × openInterest × 100 × spotPrice (sign by call/put dealer
   positioning assumption; document the convention).
2. Aggregate to: total net GEX, GEX flip point (strike where sign changes), regime
   classification (positive / negative / near-flip), and a per-strike GEX bar overlay
   on the chart (ties into A126's chart work).
3. Show in the UI: regime badge on the dashboard + GEX-by-strike on the chart tab.
   Alert when regime flips negative while short premium is on.
4. Validate: backtest short-put performance conditioned on GEX regime (preregister
   the study per A131 timestamp rules — use chain snapshots as-of the signal date,
   never revised OI).
