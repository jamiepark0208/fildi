## Why it fits fildi

  portfolio-optimizer survey) — position-level Greeks aggregation, scenario/stress.
- **License**: Apache-2.0 — permissive, no copyleft concern (unlike A104's AGPL).

## What to build (phased)
- **Phase 1 — Greeks cross-check**: run fildi's `lib/options.ts` Greeks vs GS Quant's
  pricing across a grid of strikes/expiries/IVs; file discrepancies as bugs (ties into

- **Phase 2 — adopt or wrap**: if GS Quant wins on correctness, wrap it as the reference
  pricer (Python sidecar or ported logic); if fildi's is correct, keep GS Quant as the
  regression harness in CI.
- **Phase 3 — book risk**: evaluate GS Quant's risk module for portfolio-level Greeks and
  stress scenarios on the live put-selling book.
