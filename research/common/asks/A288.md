## Ask
Convertible notes sit in debt. When the stock is above the conversion price they are equity: remove the face value
from debt and add the conversion shares (if-converted method).

## Detail
- `financials.py`: add `ConvertibleNotesPayable`, `ConvertibleDebtNoncurrent`, `ConvertibleDebtCurrent` and
  `DebtInstrumentConvertibleConversionPrice1` (USD/shares; per-instrument values are often dimensional → take the
  dimension-free total, else skip and list the name).
- `engine.inputs`: if price > conversion price → debt −= face, shares += face ÷ conversion price; record both in
  `claims` / `dilution` so the Assumptions tab shows it.
- Acceptance: SMCI, MSTR-like names in the watchlist (SMCI, RKLB, CRWV if converts) before/after with the 10-Q note
  amounts; names without converts unchanged. ≤ 10-line packet.
