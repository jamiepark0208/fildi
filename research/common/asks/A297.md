## Ask
Jamie 10-28: a friend turned ~$25k into ~$7M on SPY 0DTE calls — "a huge market drop in the AM, some confidence it reverses, near-$0 options."
Our earlier lotto tests capped winners at 10× and bought ~1 % OTM, so they could not see this payoff. Base rates (SPY daily OHLC
2018-10 →, Claude 10-28): ≥ 3 % open→low drops happen 3.6×/yr; a call struck 3 % above the drop point closed ≥ 1 % in the money on
17 % of them — but nearly all in Mar–Apr 2020 (crisis clustering). Literature: retail long 0DTE positions lose −53 % on average,
much of it spread (Beckmeyer, Branger & Gayda 2023, SSRN 4404704). Question: is there a narrow, rule-based version with positive
expected value per dollar, or is it a lottery that one day made famous?

## Detail (pre-registered)
**Trigger days:** SPY last price ≤ open × (1 − d) at checkpoint T, d ∈ {1.5 %, 2 %, 3 %}, T ∈ {10:30, 12:00, 14:00 ET}; first checkpoint that fires only.
**Trade:** buy SPY 0DTE calls struck at trigger price × (1 + k), k ∈ {1, 2, 3 %}, plus the ~0.05Δ strike. Entry = highest print in the next 5 minutes
(S053 rule; Polygon has trades, not quotes) + spread haircut sensitivity (+25 %, +50 % of entry). Hold to close, **no cap**. Alt exits: sell half at 20×.
Also the mirror (rally ≥ d → puts) as a control: a real reversal edge should not be symmetric noise.
**Filters (max 2, fixed now):** (a) SPY below its 200-day (S025's only bounce pass) · (b) S101 market state ∈ {panic, sharp_drop}.
**Data:** Polygon option minute aggs for strike ladders on trigger days only (≈ 10 strikes × trigger days, 2024-10 → now, includes Apr 2025),
`optlab.minute_bars` style cache; SPY minute closes (S052: use closes, minute h/l carry bad ticks). **One Polygon job at a time** (5/min; check
no other Polygon fetch is running). Model extension 2018-10 → 2024-09 (Mar 2020, 2022): Black–Scholes at the day's VIX × skew multipliers {1.0, 1.5, 2.0}
for far-OTM 0DTE, labelled MODEL — sizes the frequency side only, never the verdict.
**Outcome:** profit per $ risked, % of trades worthless, payoff distribution (median, 90th, max multiple), day-block bootstrap CI.
**Pass (real prints):** profit per $ > 0 with CI lower bound > −20 %, ≥ 8 trigger days, **and still > 0 after dropping the single best day**
(the "one famous day" test). Stop rule: one pass; FAIL → playbook rule "0DTE reversal lotto: negative EV, entertainment budget only" and no reruns until VIX > 30 for ≥ 5 sessions.
Smoke first: 2025-04-03/04/07/08/09 hand-checked against SPY minute closes and 3 contracts' prints.
