# A65: Stop ingesting on every Daily News poll
**From → to:** Muse → Cursor · **Date:** 2026-10-11 · **Status:** open

## Ask
`GET /desk-commentaries/daily-news` runs a full email ingest (6h window) plus book/earnings/model queries on every poll (~60s per open tab). Move ingest off the hot path.

## Detail
`artifacts/api-server/src/routes/desk-daily-news.ts` — the GET handler calls `ingestDeskCommentaries({ sinceMs: Date.now() - 6h })` on every request, and the panel polls regularly. The refresh POST already ingests a 36h window; the GET ingest is redundant and burns Gmail/Drive reads per tab per minute.
Fix: skip ingest on GET (serve from DB + cache), or gate it behind "minutes since last ingest" (e.g. ≥15 min). Keep the materiality check — it is cheap. Severity: degraded (wasted vendor/quota calls, slower responses). Acceptance: 10 rapid GETs → ≤1 ingest.
