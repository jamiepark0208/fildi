## The concept
Kaufman efficiency ratio: ER = |price change over N periods| / sum(|period-to-period changes|).
ER near 1 = clean trend; ER near 0 = chop/noise. For a put seller this is directly useful:
- Low ER (range-bound, mean-reverting tape) = premium-selling conditions are good.
- High ER (strong directional trend, especially down) = the exact regime that kills short
  puts. A101's gamma-flip filter and A56's HMM regime model both need regime inputs; ER is
  a simpler, fully transparent, cheap-to-compute complement to the HMM.
