## Study 2 — VIX mean reversion as a premium-timing overlay
The book sells premium; VIX MR says vol spikes revert. Hypothesis: put entries made in the 5 sessions after a VIX spike (VIX >= its 252d 80th pct, or +20% in 5d) keep more premium than average entries, because IV is richest exactly when reversion is likeliest — and the MM's warning is the risk leg: entries DURING the spike's first day do worse (catching the knife).
- **Data:** S003 reconstructed entries (17k puts, 2020-04→) tagged by VIX state at entry; VIX daily history.
- **Pass:** post-spike-window entries beat all-entry premium-kept by >=10% relative with no worse worst-decile tail; knife-day entries shown separately (expected worse — if they are not, the warning is wrong for this book, also a finding).
- **Ships if passed:** a VIX-state tag in the morning brief + scanner (window open/closed), display-first, sizing change only after a second confirming read.

## Study 3 — VWAP mean reversion as entry timing (intraday)
Hypothesis: selling puts intraday when the underlying is stretched below VWAP (intraday dip) vs at/above VWAP changes same-expiry outcomes. Fildi's intraday-regime code already computes VWAP separation live; this tests whether the MR prior holds for ENTRY timing on the book's horizon (not a day-trade scalp).
- **Data:** A194 1m bars (gated: needs ~60 sessions; design now, run when data exists). Interim: daily proxy using close-vs-VWAP approximations is NOT acceptable — wait for real bars.
- **Pass:** below-VWAP-timed hypothetical entries on the watchlist beat at-VWAP entries on premium-kept by >=8% at matched delta/DTE, no worse tail.

## Study 4 — Sizing rules: reverse-Martingale vs flat (money management)
The book sizes largely flat. Test on S003 history: scale contract count by entry quality (IV rank / score tercile) — the disciplined form of "size up winners" (winners = high-quality setups, known at entry, no lookahead) — vs flat sizing.
- **Pass:** quality-scaled sizing improves premium-kept per unit of worst-decile loss by >=10% with max drawdown no worse. Martingale proper (size up after losses) is NOT tested — D-tier prior + ruin math; recorded as rejected with reason.
