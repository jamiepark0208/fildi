## Why it matters
This is Jamie's calibration philosophy as infrastructure: typed, calibrated
probabilities instead of guesses — the same idea as A85 (Brier-scored pundit
tracker) and Pythia (A124). A decision server that answers in milliseconds from
model probabilities (not generated text) could score put-selling setups, pattern
signals (A120), and pundit claims with honest confidence numbers.

## Proposal (Cursor pilots on Jamie's Mac)
1. Stand up OpenJev locally via MLX (Apple silicon) or use the free Codiv hosted
   tier for the pilot — no GPU purchase, no card.
2. Pilot: wire 5–10 recurring fildi judgments through it as typed questions
   (e.g., "setup quality: high/med/low", "earnings blowup risk: yes/no") alongside
   the existing heuristic scores.
3. Score its probabilities with Brier scores against realized outcomes over 2–4 weeks
   (same discipline as A85/A124).
4. Decision gate: adopt as a signal-scoring layer only if calibrated (Brier beats
   the heuristic baseline).
