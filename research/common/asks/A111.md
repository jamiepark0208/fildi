# A111 — iv_rank minimum-history change (120 → 60 days) is undocumented

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Severity:** low

## Context
Repo-watch review of 9bee2472 ("Earnings dates: LSEG first …"). Buried in that commit,
`iv30()` in scripts/watchlist_sync/analysis/option_derive.py (~line 137) changed IV-rank
eligibility from `len(past) >= 120` to `len(past) >= 60` days of history. Neither the commit
message nor research/studies/INDEX.md mentions it.

## Why it matters
A 60-day IV rank is materially noisier than a 120-day one. This feeds S019 (true IV rank) and
the Breakdown › Options history IV-rank display — a silent methodology change in a
pre-registered study pipeline.

## Decision needed (Jamie / [quant])
- Keep 60 (more coverage for newer names) → document it in research/studies/INDEX.md and the S019 spec; or
- revert to 120.

Either way the change must be a named, dated decision, not a drive-by line in an unrelated commit.

## Acceptance
- INDEX.md records the chosen minimum-history threshold and the date/rationale.
- No other silent threshold changes in the same commit remain unaccounted.
