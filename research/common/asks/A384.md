## Ask
Fix the WLS weight for the `lse_trades` source in `stock_surface.py::fit_group`: it is `1/trades`, which gives the least-traded (least reliable) print the most influence over the fitted smile — the inverse of the quote-source weighting in the same function and of inverse-variance weighting. Weight should grow with trades, or be equal with a recorded reason.

## Detail
- Evidence: `scripts/watchlist_sync/analysis/stock_surface.py`, `fit_group()` (450cedc): quote sources get `ws.append(1.0 / spr**2)` (tighter spread → more weight); `lse_trades` gets `ws.append(1.0 / float(tr))` where `tr = max(q.trades or 1, 1)`. A 1-trade print gets 1000× the weight of a 1000-trade print. `_wls_quad` multiplies `ws` into both sides of `lstsq`, so the effective weight is `ws²` — the inversion is amplified to `1/tr²`.
- Why it matters: `merge_sources` prefers `lse_trades` over `lseg_opt_daily` whenever it has more quotes, so once the OPRA backfill (A192 Part S3, relaunched 11-08) lands, most 2025-10→ surface slices are fitted with inverted weights. The fitted skew slope `b` — the Part R input — will be driven by thin prints. The S1-surface.md report has zero `lse_trades` rows (run before the backfill), so no empirical check exists yet.
- Fix direction: `ws = float(tr)` (inverse-variance: IV-estimate variance ∝ 1/n), or equal weight if the intent is to stop liquid ATM contracts dominating the wings — but then record that reason in a comment, because it contradicts the spread-weighting logic in the same loop.
- Pass/fail: (1) S1 smoke gate still PASS after the change; (2) unit check — a high-trades ATM quote moves the fitted `a/b/c` more than a 1-trade wing quote (currently the reverse); (3) on overlapping ticker-days, fitted 30d `b` from `lse_trades` slices moves toward the chain/survey `b`; (4) regenerate S1-surface.md with `lse_trades` rows present.
- Note, not in scope: `_wls_quad` applies `ws` (not `sqrt(ws)`) so effective weights are `ws²` everywhere — same convention for all sources, leave unless [quant] says otherwise. `spot_for_group` silent `100.0` fallback and the dead `interp_cm` extrapolation guard were already noted in the S1 review (d5a47e8) — not re-filed.
