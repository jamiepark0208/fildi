## Detail (3 parts, in order)
1. **Cell tracker (week 1, cheapest, biggest lever).** Every graded rec (put engine, Model List, Strategies, S088/S172 ledgers, pattern/indicator signals) → `watchlist_sync.rec_outcomes` (rec id, source, ticker, sector, regime cell, IV÷RV tier, P(kept) tier, earnings-in-window, signal flags, P&L at real fill % of strike/debit). Beta-binomial / normal shrinkage of mean P&L per cell toward the source's base; a cell whose posterior mean < 0 with P(< 0) ≥ 0.9 and n ≥ 30 → demote (skip or half size) in put_recs/structures; shown on the rec as "this kind of trade: n, mean, odds". Re-check monthly; a demoted cell returns when the posterior crosses back.
   - Test that it would have caught: semis cell, 10-19→11-06 data only, predicts 11-09→11-13 engine semis P&L < rest. Pass = demotion would have lifted engine % of strike on the held-out weeks.
2. **Meta-label (López de Prado: primary signal says trade, secondary model says whether to take it).** Walk-forward logistic / gradient-boosted classifier on rec_outcomes features → P(win at real fill), purged + embargoed CV by expiry week. Ships only if out-of-sample Brier beats market-delta odds (S016 1.14) and top-half-by-P(win) beats bottom-half by ≥ 1 % of strike with t ≥ 2 (week-clustered). Below n for a cell → falls back to part 1.

4. **Patterns/indicators into the same ledger.** Every pattern_lab / indicator / S151 turning-point fire logged as a forecast with a graded outcome → same cell tracker → BOARD rows promote/demote from live numbers, not one-off studies.
