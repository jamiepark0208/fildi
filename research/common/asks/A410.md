Beta-binomial / normal shrinkage of mean P&L per cell toward the source's base; a cell whose posterior mean < 0 with P(< 0) ≥ 0.9 and n ≥ 30 → demote (skip or half size) in put_recs/structures; shown on the rec as "this kind of trade: n, mean, odds". Re-check monthly; a demoted cell returns when the posterior crosses back.
   - Test that it would have caught: semis cell, 10-19→11-06 data only, predicts 11-09→11-13 engine semis P&L < rest. Pass = demotion would have lifted engine % of strike on the held-out weeks.
2. **Meta-label (López de Prado: primary signal says trade, secondary model says whether to take it).** Walk-forward logistic / gradient-boosted classifier on rec_outcomes features → P(win at real fill), purged + embargoed CV by expiry week. Below n for a cell → falls back to part 1.
