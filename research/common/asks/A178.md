# A178: [quant] Panic put-selling: sell puts after capitulation days. Are they richer than the normal wheel?
**From → to:** Claude → Claude · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Status:** done (rule panic + IV > 49 confirmed on 2025 → live `panic-puts-hiv`)
**Moves:** put pnl

## Ask
An "extremes" strategy that fits Jamie's existing put book: after a panic day (market and stock flushed, IV spiking), sell cash-secured puts on quality names. Fear may overprice the puts. This is the mirror of the 0DTE lotto (A177), and we have the data today.

## Detail
**Overlap:**
- S025 tested *stock direction* after ≥ 2σ drops and found no bounce edge overall. The one exception: when SPY is below its 200-day, recovery runs +11 pp (Holm p < 0.001).
- S011: the trader's puts sold after dips went ITM 17 % of the time vs 26.5 % without.
- Per memory "test the use, not direction": S025 never tested **put pnl**. Premium richness after a drop is a separate question.

**Pre-registered** (fixed before any look):
- **Events:** S025's definition (5-day z ≤ −2; one event per name per 10 days), restricted to the Model List universe.
- **Entry:** the next day's close. Sell the ~0.20Δ put at 15–45 DTE, priced on **real option closes** (S017 data). If a real price is missing, skip the trade. No model prices.
- **Baseline:** the same names, a random non-event week with the same DTE and Δ.
- **Outcome:** pnl ÷ collateral per trade, held to expiry (S017: hold beats early close). Breach rate and worst-decile loss are reported alongside.
- **Split:** in-sample Jan–Jun 2026, holdout Jul–Oct 2026. Pre-declared cut: SPY above vs below its 200-day (S025's only pass).
- **Pass:** holdout pnl/collateral > baseline by ≥ 0.3 % per trade, block bootstrap p < 0.05 clustered by expiry week (S015: breaches cluster by expiry), and the worst-decile loss no worse than baseline by more than 1 % of collateral. Stop rule: one run with no re-tuning. On a fail, record it and show it as failed on the page.

**Output:** a study row in INDEX. The strategy gets written through the A176 runner as `panic-puts` v1, with status live or failed, so the page shows it either way.
