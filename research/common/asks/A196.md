# A196: [quant] Study — Visser trend/momentum filter for put entries

**From → to:** Muse → Claude · **Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Status:** open
**Source:** Jordi Visser (ex–Morgan Stanley MD, ex-president of a $4B hedge fund) on Titans of Tomorrow podcast, reel sent by Jamie 10-20. His 5-agent research-team pitch maps to fildi's existing stack — not the ask. The ask is his concrete entry rule.
**Expanded 2026-10-20 (Jamie feedback):** study orders alone waste tokens. Below: Muse's own analysis, ablations, trade-off quantification, data dependencies, downstream paths, and a step where Claude proposes additional use cases before running.

## The rule (his words, paraphrased tight)
Only trade names: (1) above the 200-day MA, (2) with the 200-day MA sloping up, (3) where rate-of-change momentum has just reset near zero after being higher — a pullback inside a confirmed uptrend — then ride the ~3-month trend.

## Study design (pre-registered, A51 pattern)
- Put-selling entry filter on S003 reconstructed puts: filtered vs unfiltered, same names/eras.
- **Ablation (required):** test (1)+(2) alone vs (1)+(2)+(3). If the plain uptrend filter captures everything, Visser's momentum-reset adds nothing and we adopt the simpler rule.
- ROC window pre-registered at 63d; no window shopping. "Reset near zero" pre-defined as ROC crossing from >+5% to within ±1% in the prior 10 sessions — Claude may tighten the definition in review but must fix it before seeing results.
- Metrics: premium kept %, assignment rate, expiry ITM rate, worst-decile 5d move.
- **Entries-kept % (required):** what fraction of historical entries the filter blocks. Quality gain at 70% blocked volume can still lose money. Pass requires entries-kept ≥40%.
- Pass: premium-kept +≥10% relative OR assignments −20% relative, no worse tail, entries-kept ≥40%. Era split pre/post 2024 (A131).

## First/second-level analysis (Muse)
- Prior: uptrend filters mechanically cut assignments (fewer downtrend entries) but cut premium volume; the study exists to price that trade, not assume it.
- Redundancy check: correlate filter decisions with fildi's existing setup.py S/R + IV-rank behavior. If the Visser filter agrees with existing discretionary filters 90% of the time, its marginal value is codification (still useful — it makes Jamie's eyeball rule automatic), not new alpha. Report agreement rate.
- Payoff-shape caveat: Visser's rule was designed for LONG STOCK over ~3 months. Transplanted to 30–45 DTE put selling, the holding period is shorter than his trend horizon — the filter is an entry-quality screen here, not a trend-riding system. Expectations set accordingly.

## Upstream data — dependencies & maintenance

- No new feeds, no new maintenance: inputs already exist; if adopted, the filter is three columns on existing daily bars.

## Downstream impact

- If only (1)+(2) passes: adopt the simple uptrend gate, credit the ablation.
- If fail: result still feeds A134/A56 regime work as a tested-and-rejected state variable (negative results are inputs, not waste).
