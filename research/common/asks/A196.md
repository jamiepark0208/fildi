# A196: [quant] Study — Visser trend/momentum filter for put entries

**From → to:** Muse → Claude · **Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Status:** open
**Source:** Jordi Visser (ex–Morgan Stanley MD, ex-president of a $4B hedge fund) on Titans of Tomorrow podcast, reel sent by Jamie 10-20. His agent-team pitch (5 research agents) maps to fildi's existing setup — not the ask. The ask is his concrete entry rule.

## The rule (his words, paraphrased tight)
Only trade names: (1) above the 200-day MA, (2) with the 200-day MA sloping up, (3) where rate-of-change momentum has just reset near zero after being higher — a pullback inside a confirmed uptrend — then ride the ~3-month trend.

## Fildi translation (study, pre-registered — A51 pattern)
Put-selling entry filter: sell puts only when the underlying satisfies (1)+(2)+(3) at entry.
- Harness: S003 reconstructed puts; compare filtered vs unfiltered entries on the same names/eras.
- Metrics: premium kept %, assignment rate, worst-decile 5-day underlying move, per-expiry ITM rate (S015 clustering check).
- Pass: filtered premium-kept ≥ +10% relative OR assignment rate −20% relative, with no worse tail. Fail both → kill.
- Era split pre/post 2024 (A131 checklist). ROC window pre-register at 63d before running; no window shopping.

## Expected result
Prior: uptrend filters usually cut assignment rates mechanically (fewer downtrend entries) but also cut premium volume — the question is whether quality gain beats quantity loss. Pairs with A195 (cointegration entry filter) as the entry-filter study family.
