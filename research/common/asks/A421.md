## Ask
`a372_call_exits.py` prices covered-call exits with the wrong day-count convention since c7ef91f47c, so the shipped A372 call-exits packet numbers are systematically off. Fix the year fraction, re-run, regenerate the packet, and state the bp/wk delta.

## Detail
- Evidence (repo-watch 2026-11-26, branch `cursor/mac-dev`): c7ef91f47c replaced the local parity `bs_call` with `quantcore.bs.bs_call_px` and changed the time inputs from `tt0 = hold / 252` / `tt = dleft / 252` to `years=years_from_days(HOLD)` / `years=years_from_days(dleft)` (`scripts/watchlist_sync/analysis/a372_call_exits.py`, `call_pnl_path`).
- `hold` / `dleft` are session counts (`ST = float(c[i + hold])` indexes daily closes; the pre-change code divided by 252). `quantcore/bs.py` documents the rule: "Use `years_from_days(days)` (calendar days / 365) or `years_from_sessions(n)` (trading sessions / 252). Never mix the two." `years_from_days` on a session count understates years by 252/365 ≈ 0.69× — at a 5-session hold, 0.0198y becomes 0.0137y, so call premia run ~17% low (√time). Both `prem0` and every `prem_j` cover price are affected, so the packet's hold and early-exit bp/wk are both biased, not just re-based.
- The packet `research/studies/out/a372-call-exits.md` was refreshed after the switch (794462f599 "Refresh A372 call-exits packet after quantcore.bs pricing"), so the published numbers embed the wrong convention.
- Scope check: `a372_exits.py` (put exits) prices from real bid/ask fills, not BS, and `a372_book.py` only imports `quantcore.ta` — the bug is confined to the two `bs_call_px` call sites in `a372_call_exits.py`.
- Fix: `years=years_from_sessions(HOLD)` and `years=years_from_sessions(dleft)`; re-run `a372_call_exits.py` (incl. `--smoke`) and regenerate the packet.
- Pass/fail: packet regenerated from the corrected pricer; the file (or the A372 ask note) states the before/after bp/wk delta for hold and each exit; add a pinning check that a 5-session call prices at the 252-day year fraction (e.g. assert `bs_call_px(..., years=years_from_sessions(5))` matches the pre-c7ef91f47c parity value to within rounding, or a unit test on `call_pnl_path` prem0).
