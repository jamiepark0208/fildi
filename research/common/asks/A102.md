# A102 — Periodic extreme-score review (outlier QA for score models)

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Severity:** medium

## Context
Jamie asked (Oct 14): periodically review the most extreme outlier scores (fundamental,
technical, option, stock, fair value) to catch model bugs — wrong golden source, wrong weights,
sector peer mapping errors. Web research (model validation practices) + score-audit inventory
completed Oct 15.

## Research headline
- The extreme-score review is regulator-sanctioned QA: the Fed's SR 11-7 guidance names **outlier
  analysis explicitly** as outcomes analysis. Three pillars: conceptual soundness, outcomes
  analysis, ongoing monitoring — plus "effective challenge" and benchmarking against a challenger.
- **One-person cadence**: per-run automated flags + weekly ~20-min extreme-score review
  (top/bottom 10s, movers, divergences, conflicts) with a disposition log; monthly ~1-hr
  monitoring pack (distributions, drift, overrides, peer-map changes, weight sensitivity);
  annual half-day light validation (conceptual review + naive challenger + outcomes spot check).
- **Input attribution**: for weighted scores, exact weight×standardized-input decomposition —
  attribute the extreme score, identify the dominant input, validate that input across the universe.
- **Peer-group validation**: ISS-style checks — size band (revenue 0.4–2.5×), 12–24 peers,
  >3σ score-vs-peer divergence flags, leave-one-peer-out robustness, annual re-map review with
  change log (GICS gotchas: Amazon/Tesla = Consumer Discretionary, 2018 reclassification moved
  >8% of the S&P 500).
- **Golden-source audit**: divergence dashboard (conflict rate by vendor × field, who won and by
  which rule), vendor accuracy track record vs ground truth, manual-override log reviewed
  monthly (frequent/better-than-model overrides → revise the rule), per-score lineage to vendor.
- Standing artifacts: one-page model inventory per score model, survivorship rules, peer-map
  change log, override log, disposition log.
- Caveat: SR 26-02 reportedly replaced SR 11-7 (unverified); PSI cutoffs are conventions.

## Fildi today (inventory)
- **Gap 1**: db-quality-audit checks feed health (stale feeds, 40% jumps, orphans) but **never
  score plausibility** — no distribution or outlier checks at all.
- **Gap 2**: Snapshot tables store everything needed (daily scores, topMetrics+contrib,
  peerGroupId, inputsDigest) but nothing lists top extremes or WoW jumps — nobody looks.
- **Gap 3**: Fair-value trimming hides repeat-offender voters (no systematic-bias tracking);
  peer-health-check flags misclassification but never asks "did the score jump because the peer
  group is wrong"; divergence is a count, not a review queue.
- **Reusable**: db-quality-audit cron (extend with score checks; files severity-tagged ASKS rows);
  snapshot tables (raw material for jump/extreme lists); peer-health-check.ts (extend with
  score-swing-on-regroup); field_provenance.divergent (top-divergent review list);
  fund-score-guards.ts (in-engine guard pattern for output-level rules).

## What to build (phased)
- **Phase 1 — automated flags**: per-run checks on every score snapshot write: |z|>3 vs own
  history, WoW Δ beyond ~2σ, extreme-decile score with median inputs, missing/stale inputs,
  vendor conflict on dominant inputs. Each flag carries one-sentence why-flagged context.
- **Phase 2 — weekly extreme review**: top/bottom 10 per score type + biggest movers + top
  divergent fields, rendered as a review list with disposition log (confirmed bug → ASKS row;
  explained → note; false positive → tune flag).
- **Phase 3 — peer + golden-source audits**: score-swing-on-regroup check in peer-health;
  divergence dashboard (conflict rate by vendor × field); override log with monthly review.
- **Phase 4 — monthly pack + annual validation**: distributions, drift (PSI), weight
  sensitivity, naive challenger benchmark for each score model.
