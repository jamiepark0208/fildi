Deadline per call 20 s. Add to `scripts/watchlist_sync/daily.py` (after fundamentals, non-blocking) and data_freshness.
2. **API** `GET /api/stocks/:ticker/congress` (last 3 y rows + 90 d / 1 y buy vs sell counts and $ midpoint totals) and `GET /api/congress/latest?days=30&universe=1` (newest disclosures for universe names, plus top names by net $ bought 90 d). Stored data only.
3. **UI** (a) Breakdown: `components/breakdown/congress-section.tsx` placed near the insider/ownership area in stock-breakdown.tsx — compact table (txn date · disclosed · lag · member · chamber · buy/sell · amount) + one summary line; (b) a "Congress" panel on the Feed page (pages/feed.tsx) or Macro › news area — latest disclosures in the universe + "most bought 90 d" table.
