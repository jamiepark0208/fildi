# A220 — [quant] IV-wall coverage calibration: strike floor + wall-test defense (study)
Filed 2026-10-20 by Muse → Claude. Source: @atraintrades "IV walls" reel (expected range from HV + contract pricing; ~90% containment; reversal trades at wall tests), re-analyzed at Jamie's direction after Muse's first pass dismissed it too cheaply.

## 1st-level analysis (Muse)
A "90% band" is a ±1.645σ construction, or a narrower IV band whose realized coverage is inflated by the variance risk premium (SPX: RV/IV ~0.87 average). Coverage therefore varies by ticker and regime: at RV/IV = 0.85 a ±1.645σ_IV band realizes ~94.7% coverage (one-sided breach 2.65% vs 5% priced — puts at that strike carry ~1.9x the breach frequency in their price); at RV/IV = 1.15 it realizes ~84.7% (breach 7.65% vs 5% priced — puts ~1.5x underpriced in frequency terms). The vendor's reversal scalp does NOT inherit the 90%: coverage counts closes, trades trigger on touches (reflection principle: P(touch) ~ 2x P(close beyond)), payoff is asymmetric on breach/trend days, and a touch itself signals today's realized vol is running hot. The edge, if any, is calibration + placement + defense — below.

## Use cases / studies (Claude: propose additional ones before running)
**H1 — Coverage-calibrated strike floor (data in hand).** Per ticker, rolling-252d realized coverage of IV bands (ex-event days separated; event windows price jumps, not diffusion). On S003-reconstructed entries, tag coverage tercile at entry + strike position vs the smile-adjusted wall.
- Pass: over-covered tercile beats universe premium-kept per unit worst-decile loss by >=10%; AND inside-wall entries show >=15% worse worst-decile outcomes than outside-wall at matched premium. Either leg passing ships that leg.
- Ships: (i) per-ticker coverage score in the scanner (over-covered = rich for selling; under-covered = gate / strike floor moves out); (ii) wall floor rule — no short put inside the smile-adjusted 90% wall for its expiry without an override tag; (iii) A213 panel companion: smile-implied breach % beside empirical P(OTM), gap labeled as coverage premium.
- Both legs fail: the IV-wall concept is retired for strike purposes on evidence; A137 remains the vol lens.

**H2 — Wall-test defense for open shorts (data-gated).** Event study on A194 1m bars (needs >=60 sessions; SPY interim if backfill allows): P(close beyond wall) by touch ordinal (1st vs 2nd), touch time, day-type stack (VWAP + OR low + prior-day low already lost?). Simulate management rules (A136 what-if protocol): e.g. close/roll on second touch before 14:00 ET vs hold baseline.
- Pass: 2nd-touch continuation >=55% vs 1st-touch <=40%; best rule cuts worst-decile episode losses >=20% at <=10% premium-kept cost.
- Ships: intraday alert + standing rule in the book brief ("second wall test on XYZ — roll trigger armed").
