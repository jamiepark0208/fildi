# A126 — Order-flow-style indicators on stock breakdown charts (volume profile, VWAP, volume)

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-16
**Severity:** medium

## Context
Jamie shared @fibsandflow's reel (order-flow charting: volume profile, bid/ask delta,
VWAP bands, EMA, DOM) and wants "indicators like this" on fildi's stock breakdown
charts, plus general chart UI improvement.

## What the charts have today (verified 2026-10-16)
- `price-chart.tsx` (lightweight-charts): candles, EMA 50/200 overlays, S/R zones
  (static + dashed MA/Bollinger), markers, price lines.
- Backend `indicators.ts`: RSI, MFI, ATR, MACD cross, Stochastic — computed but shown
  as numbers, not chart overlays.
- **Missing entirely:** volume histogram, volume profile, VWAP, delta/footprint, DOM.

## Honest data constraint (do not skip this)
The reel's tools are order-flow: delta/footprint and DOM need tick-level data fildi
doesn't have (daily Schwab+Yahoo bars). Do NOT plan those — they'd be fake.
What transfers honestly to daily bars:
1. **Volume histogram** — trivial; daily volume already in DB. Biggest visual gap.
2. **Volume profile (POC / VAH / VAL)** — computable on daily bars over a lookback
   (coarse but valid). Render as horizontal histogram at right of chart.
3. **Anchored VWAP** — from recent swing highs/lows (daily-anchored, not session).
   Useful mean-reversion levels for a put seller.

## Proposal (Cursor builds)
1. Volume histogram sub-pane under the price chart (toggleable).
2. Volume profile overlay: POC line + value-area band, N-day lookback selector
   (20/60/120d), toggleable.
3. Anchored VWAP: auto-anchor at the two most recent swing high/low; toggleable lines.
4. Indicator toggle panel: clean checklist UI (the reel's dark-theme clarity is the
   bar) — every overlay on/off without reload.
5. Keep the existing legend explanations (they're good); extend to new indicators.

## Out of scope
- Bid/ask delta, footprint charts, DOM/tape — need tick data; explicitly not planned.
- No new data vendors for this; daily bars only.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Volume bars, volume profile (POC/VAH/VAL), anchored VWAP all render and toggle.
- [ ] No perf regression on chart load (profile computed server-side or memoized).
- [ ] Jamie eyeball-approves the new chart UI.
