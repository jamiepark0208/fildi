# A139 — ICT indicator suite + rank-weighted composite score

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-16
**Severity:** medium

## Context
From @abeteddymaruta's indicator tier-list reel. Full 1–10 ranking extracted
frame-by-frame (all high confidence): 1. AMD model (accumulation/manipulation/
distribution), 2. Liquidity sweep, 3. Order flow, 4. IFVG, 5. FVG,
6. Supply & demand, 7. EMA, 8. RSI, 9. Fibonacci, 10. MACD.
Jamie's directive: treat ranked trader opinions as priors for fildi's indicator
weights — hypotheses to validate against data, not gospel.

## Why it matters
The ranking says ICT/order-flow concepts beat classic oscillators. Fildi today
computes RSI, MACD, Stochastic, ATR, MFI, EMA 50/200, S/R zones — but NONE of the
top 6 (no FVG/IFVG detection, no liquidity-sweep detection, no AMD phase
classification, no footprint/order-flow). The composite score is weighted toward
indicators the crowd ranks lowest.

## Proposal (Cursor builds)
1. Implement detectors for the missing top-6: FVG/IFVG (3-candle imbalance with
   fill tracking), liquidity sweep (wick beyond prior high/low + close back
   inside), AMD phase classifier (accumulation/manipulation/distribution via
   range + volume signature), supply/demand zones (rally-base-drop / drop-base-
   rally). Daily bars first; intraday when A126 lands.
2. Composite score with rank-derived prior weights (linear by rank, normalized):
   AMD 18%, Liquidity sweep 16%, Order flow 15%, IFVG 13%, FVG 11%, S&D 9%,
   EMA 7%, RSI 5%, Fib 4%, MACD 2%. "Order flow" maps to A126's volume-profile/
   delta work until footprint charts exist.
3. Validate: backtest the composite vs equal-weight and vs the current scorer on
   the 1,513-trade record; re-estimate weights from data (the ranking is the
   prior, the data is the posterior). Preregister per A131.

## Out of scope
- Footprint charts (needs tick data; A126 covers the roadmap).
- Treating the ranking as fixed truth — weights must be re-fit.
