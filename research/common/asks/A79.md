## Context
Jamie asked (Oct 14) that the market recaps wire into fildi instead of living as prose. Idea 1 of 3:
a per-ticker earnings risk score surfaced on the earnings calendar, flagging BAC-type blowup risk
before the print with auto-size-small guidance. Builds directly on the A51/A67 event-window study
(1,513 Robinhood trades): that study measures the realized gap distribution; this row turns it into a
live pre-earnings input.

## What to build
1. **Risk score per (ticker, earnings date)**: inputs = historical earnings-move distribution vs
   priced move (ATM straddle), IV percentile, recent analyst-revision direction, sector cohort stress
   (e.g. SOX 6-day slide). Output: 0–100 blowup-risk score + sized guidance (skip / half-size / normal).
   Score must cite its drivers (top 2 contributors shown in UI).
2. **UI**: badge on the earnings calendar and on the options scanner row for the ticker; hover/click
   shows the driver breakdown. A "high risk" badge must be impossible to miss pre-market.
3. **Backend**: nightly + intraday refresh job; score recomputed when IV or the priced move shifts
   materially. Log score, drivers, decision, outcome → feeds A74 calibration and A80 thesis tracker.
4. **Guardrails**: never blocks a trade (advisory only); deterministic display, probabilistic inputs
   (per A74 architecture).

## Reviews

**Claude plan 2026-10-14 (narrowed; this one has real value):**
*Point:* the Model List **blocks** every put with earnings before expiry (`build_plan.py` `no_earnings_before_expiry`, severity block). The evidence splits:
- S003 (17k backtest puts at the model's usual distance): earnings in the window raises blow-ups.
- S015 (the trader's 935 puts): earnings puts did *better* (+1.42% vs +0.23%, ITM 5% vs 23%), because he went wider (z 1.69 vs 1.1).

The variable that reconciles them is **strike distance vs this stock's own earnings moves**. That's the score worth building, not a 4-input 0–100 blend. Four inputs on a few hundred earnings events would overfit (METHODS #6), and A51's "1,513-trade event study" was never run (A51 declined).
1. **One number per (ticker, earnings date, strike):** OTM % ÷ p90 of the stock's past |earnings reaction| (close before → close after, last 8–12 reports; `prices_historical` + `earnings_calendar` / LSEG events). Show it as "strike is outside 9 of the last 10 earnings moves", plus the market's priced move (ATM straddle from `option_chain_history`) for comparison.
2. **Study first (pre-register in studies/INDEX.md):** on S003 puts with earnings in the window, breach rate and P&L by bucket of that ratio (< 0.75, 0.75–1.25, > 1.25). Stop rule: no monotone breach-rate gradient with week-cluster CI → display only.
3. **If it passes:** replace the blanket earnings block with "block only when the strike is inside the stock's p75 earnings move". This is a selection change, so before/after + user approval.
4. **Where:** Model List row chip `ER Thu AMC · outside 9/10 moves`; Live › Portfolio Alerts › Your names for held puts. Advisory sizing text ("skip / half / normal") is dropped until the study supports it.
