## Detail

### Goal
1. **MAC-PRIVATE.** Anything that reads personal holdings or transactions: `portfolio_*` tables, `pm_book_latest`, `pm_alerts`, rbh-export/ingest, Robinhood CSVs, `~/.fildi/private.md`. Also Schwab-token-dependent jobs, LSEG Workspace (`:9000`), WRDS (Duo), and the OpenJev local model.
2. **PUBLIC/PORTABLE.** News/desk ingest, event-bullets, earnings backfill, fmp-analyst-feed, voices, option-scores, scorecard/technical/fundamental updates, regime checks, transcripts, option-history. Runnable as one-shot commands on any Linux host against a DB that has no private tables.
