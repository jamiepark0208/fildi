## Research headline
- **Reverse DCF is the standard**: fix WACC/terminal growth → set model value = target price → solve
  for implied revenue growth / terminal margin / competitive-advantage period (bisection; value is
  monotonic). Run it on the *consensus target* to express "our FV ≠ street" as implied-assumption
  differences ("street implies 12% CAGR vs our 8%").
- **Decompose gaps** with a sequential ceteris-paribus waterfall (growth → margin → WACC → terminal),
  Shapley-averaged over orderings; complement with tornado sensitivity.
- **Test changes rigorously**: price-convergence hit rates at 6/12/24m, V/P-quintile efficacy
  (Frankel & Lee 1998), Diebold–Mariano for pairwise model comparison, walk-forward only, strict
  train/val/test. Note: beating analyst consensus is a low bar — they're "highly inaccurate and
  biased" long-term.
- **Consensus data**: FMP per-analyst timestamped target feeds (best for point-in-time series; verify
  paid-tier gating), Finnhub price-target, Yahoo snapshot-only fallback. StarMine has no history.
- **Viz rules**: point-in-time series only; 12-month horizon matching; consensus band + our FV + price;
  shade divergence beyond 15–20%; fade stale (>12m) estimates.
