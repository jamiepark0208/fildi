## Ask
Jamie 11-03, how he trades:
- He checks oversold/overbought, whether price is near support (low) or resistance (high), and whether the stock crashed or rallied over the last 3–6 weeks. Then he picks sell or long, puts or calls.
- Reverse-engineer it: start from every real low/high (intraday, week, month, 3 months), find what the technicals looked like near it, then backtest single, 2-factor and multi-factor signals.
- Selling deep OTM can be looser ("we can set the strike"). Long options need tighter, stricter signals (they can expire worthless).
- Execution matters: timing, when to close, holding period.

## Detail
**Data ($0 vendor, all stored):**
- Daily: `lseg_research.daily_history` (565 names, 2016→, OHLCV + iv30), sector ETFs (`sector_etf_daily`).
- Intraday: `optlab.bars_5m` (220 names, 2024-10→) + `breadth_5m`.
- Options: `optlab.bars`, `option_chain_history`, `skew_survey`.
- Big sample = history, clustered by month. No waiting on live.

**Phase 1: labels (forward-only, every day, every name).** Horizons: intraday (rest of day), week (5 sessions), month (21), quarter (63).
- **Good low at h:** the next h bars never trade > 0.25 ATR below today's low AND rally ≥ k·ATR(h) above the close (mirror for good high).
- Also the path: max adverse and max favorable excursion, h-return.
- **Never** a window with past bars in the label (the S082 trap).
- Per-trade labels:
  - sell put = no close below spot × (1 − z·σ√h) before expiry (loose, zone);
  - long call = close at h ≥ breakeven (strict, point);
  - mirrors for calls.

**Phase 2: reverse-engineer (hypothesis generation, fit window 2016–2021 only).**
- Take the true lows/highs per horizon and snapshot features at t−5..t.
- Compare with look-alike days (same drawdown × 20-d return quintile, not extremes).
- Output: candidate features + thresholds ranked by separation. Hypotheses only; nothing is graded here.

**Feature families (each cites its source):**
- oversold/overbought: RSI2/14, stochastic, Bollinger %b, z5;
- support/resistance: prior pivot lows/highs, volume-by-price nodes, anchored VWAP from the last swing, MA50/200 and 52-w distance, prior-day/week high-low;
- relative move: 15/30-session return vs SPY and sector ETF, beta-residual;
- volume: volume z, capitulation bar, up/down volume, close location in range;
- volatility: IV÷RV, IV rank, IV change, term slope;
- options: skew where stored, GEX (SPY/QQQ);
- regime: SPY state, breadth.

**Phase 3: prospective test (every day, forward-only).**
- Lift tables per feature (skill fildi-pattern-research tiers), then all 2-factor pairs among the top 15 candidates, then 3+ via confluence count and one HistGradientBoosting model.
- Fit 2016–2022, check 2023–24, holdout 2025→ once.
- Benjamini–Hochberg across the pair grid; same sign in both halves; month-block t.

**Phase 4: trade + execution, per instrument, on stored quotes / the S081 engine.** Signal deciles → trades:
- sell put 0.08/0.12/0.20Δ;
- long call 0.50/0.30Δ, 14–45 DTE;
- sell call; long put.

Execution grid:
- entry at signal close vs next open vs intraday confirmation (5-m reclaim of VWAP, 2024-10→ only);
- exits: hold to expiry, take-profit 50 %/100 %, stop −50 %, time stop at ½ DTE, signal-flip exit.

Report per cell: n, n months, win, mean and median P&L per $, worst 5 %, month-block t, ±25 % cost stress.

**Strictness by instrument (pass bars, pre-registered):**
- **Sell puts/calls:**
  - breach rate ≤ base − 3 pts AND mean P&L ≥ base, t ≥ 2, both halves;
  - a weak/useful lift tier is enough (zone call).
- **Long calls/puts:**
  - strong tier (≥ 1.5× lift) AND mean P&L per $ debit > 0 after fills with t ≥ 2 AND survives +25 % cost AND holdout same sign;
  - anything less = "context only, not tradeable".

**Stop rules:**
- A phase that finds nothing at a horizon stops that horizon (reported, not hidden).
- No post-hoc feature swaps into the holdout.
- Smoke first: 10 names, 2 horizons, one hand-checked label + one hand-checked trade.

**Outputs:**
- `out/opt/S151.md` + INDEX row.
- Per-name daily "turning-point score" by horizon (context in Breakdown first).
- The passing (instrument × signal × exit) cells as A327 shadow challengers. Product change only with the before/after.
