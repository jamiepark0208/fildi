# A81 — Put-seller "so what" in the options scanner

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Severity:** medium

## Context
Jamie asked (Oct 14) that the market recaps wire into fildi. Idea 3 of 3: every big single-name
mover gets a one-line premium-seller take next to the price in the options scanner — not buried in
a recap. BAC −8.2% on a revenue miss + $1.2B fine should read as "IV spike un-sellable, wait for
crush" right on the scanner row.

## What to build
1. **Mover detection**: intraday scan flags tickers with |move| > threshold (tune: 5%) or volume
   > 2× average; enrich with the driver (earnings, downgrade, macro) from existing news/event feeds.
2. **"So what" line**: one sentence per mover, put-seller lens: is the premium sellable now, or is
   it gap-risk? Examples: post-earnings crush → "sell the crush, half size"; −10% on downgrade →
   "gap risk, wait"; sector-wide slide → "index spreads over single-name".
   Deterministic templates fed by the event classifier — no free-text LLM in the hot path.
3. **UI**: new column/expandable row in the options scanner; also surfaced on the ticker's detail
   view. Dismissible per ticker per day.
4. **Backend**: reuse A79's event-risk inputs where available; log the take + outcome for A80-style
   grading later.
