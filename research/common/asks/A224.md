## Why this tool (what the first pass missed)
1. **Shadow Account:** upload a broker journal → it extracts the rules you ACTUALLY trade by ("shadow strategy"), replays them against your actual trades, and produces behavior diagnostics + an audit report. Fildi already stores Jamie's Robinhood exports, so the input exists. This is the most personal fit in the whole reel batch: stated strategy vs traded strategy, diffed.
2. **Alpha zoo bench:** 478 published alphas (Qlib Alpha158 commit-pinned, Kakushadze 101, GTJA 191, academic, PIT-safe fundamentals) benchable on your universe in one command, categorized alive / reversed / dead (IC-based). Jamie's rule: ranked formulas are priors to validate — this is the honest, licensed version of the "100k strategies" corpus A203 found did not exist. Output = a shortlist for A196/A204-style entry filters, not signals.
3. **options_portfolio engine:** multi-leg, Greeks, payoff/scenario — a second-opinion backtester for the Structures work (S075/S076 shipped on fildi's own stack). Scope limit, from their docs: options data is Yahoo chains, and NO assignment/early-exercise modeling is documented — valid for structure ranking, payoff shape, Greek paths; NOT for assignment-risk truth. Any number that would change a decision gets re-run on Schwab data in fildi.
4. **Quantlib multipletesting module:** deflated significance / purged CV, callable via CLI/MCP without adopting the platform — with ~20 studies in flight, false-discovery control is a real gap in fildi's pass/fail bars.
