## Why these choices (for Jamie)
- **Re-strike instead of drop:** the edge is in selling rich premium at a target probability (delta) — that survives a 3 % move once the strike moves with the price. Dropping on drift throws away an idea whose probability is unchanged.
- **Carry rate ≥ 70 % bar:** a flat week should give a mostly stable list; 70 % leaves room for real changes (earnings, edge loss). It's a stability target, not a P&L claim — part 2's replay also reports that the carried ideas' graded pnl ≥ the fresh picks', so stability doesn't keep bad ideas.
- **Long options need a setup:** S076 says buying options unconditionally loses (you pay the spread and the volatility premium). Jamie's view (long calls now, long puts 2–3 weeks ago) is a *timing* claim, so the study tests timing setups against the unconditional baseline, on data it hasn't seen (holdout), with t ≥ 2 so one lucky stretch can't pass.
