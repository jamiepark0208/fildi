## Ask
Build a research-trigger library for the daily market briefs: when a headline or desk flag matches a known transmission, the brief answers it with history — what happened the last N times — instead of a verdict. Seed it with the two triggers Jamie named on Nov 12 (CEO scandal at a held name; Japan PPI hot → carry trade), then extend the taxonomy. Each trigger is a small pre-registered study: prior instances, base rates at 1-day / 1-week / 1-month / 3-month windows, and the comp-set split that decides which base rate applies. The brief cron consults the library; no trigger ships a bullet without its base rate.

## Detail
### 1st-level analysis (what Muse verified Nov 12, before filing — the two seed studies are DONE, the library is the build)
- **CEO scandal / sudden resignation (NVDA, Huang resigned Nov 12):** 11 CEO-scandal cases 2005–2025: 6 of 11 higher at 3 months, 5 of 11 beat the S&P 500, 8 of 10 above their first-day close. 1-year: Amazon Jan 2019 +14.8%, McDonald's Nov 2019 +14.6%, Boeing Mar 2005 +25.3%, Tesla Jul 2022 −1.2%, Microsoft May 2021 +5.4%, Intel Jun 2018 −11.8%, HP Aug 2010 −33.5%. The average is useless — the split is the signal: founder-succession-vacuum cases (Intel 2018 −13.8% at 3mo, HP 2010 −33.5% at 1y) vs clean-succession cases (McDonald's 2019 +11.0% at 3mo, Boeing 2005 +10.3%). NVDA's own proxy admits dependence on Huang ("highly dependent on the services of our longstanding executive team") — the Intel/HP side of the distribution is the relevant comp, not the average. Sources: Barchart Nov 9 2026 (MCD $194.48 → $217.44 +11.8% in a year; INTC $49.71 → $47.87 −3.7%); social-video 3-month tabulations.
- **Japan PPI hot → carry trade (Japan PPI Oct +7.1% y/y vs +6.5% exp, import prices +24.8%):** transmission is hot PPI → Dec BOJ hike odds → yen strength → carry unwind → forced selling of US risk assets. BOJ already hiked twice in 2026 (Jun, Sep) to 1.25%; a third would be the first 3-hikes-in-a-year since 1989; 54 of 78 economists in an Oct Reuters poll expect a Dec hike. Precedent: Aug 2024 — BOJ +15bp Jul 31, weak US jobs Aug 2 (114K vs 175K exp), Nikkei −12.4% Aug 5 (worst since 1987), S&P −3%, VIX 65 intraday / 38.6 close, dollar 161 → 141.70 yen in 3 weeks, CFTC yen shorts ~180K contracts flipped net long within 2 weeks. Counter-case that disciplines the trigger: Sep 2026 BOJ hiked 1% → 1.25% but the Fed hiked first, the differential didn't compress, yen weakened, US tech rose — no unwind. Current state: BOJ 1.25%, Fed 3.50–3.75%, yen ~160, CFTC yen shorts ~92K (~half the 2024 peak, rebuilding), ~$500B carry positions (Morgan Stanley), Japan holds ~$1.07T in Treasuries. The trigger fires on differential compression + crowded positioning, not on the PPI print alone.

### 2nd-level analysis (what it means for fildi)
- The Nov 12 morning brief reported both events and explained neither: NVDA got a resignation line with no base rate (Jamie owns the stock); Japan PPI got a desk-flag line with no mechanism (why does a Japanese producer-price print move his US put book?). A brief that lists events without base rates is a news feed, not a desk note — and the template's "Why it matters" headers forced verdict-shaped filler instead of research. The Nov 12 template redesign (both cron bodies) killed the headers; this library supplies what replaces them: one bullet per trigger, with numbers.
- The library is also the missing step 1 of the Oct 21 news-base playbook: today a negative-press trigger means "review the name in fildi" by hand; with the library it means "review the name against its trigger's base rate," which is faster and pre-registered, so it can't be gamed after the fact.

### Use cases (with why + expected value)
1. **CEO-event bullet with the right comp set (build first — NVDA is live).** When a CEO scandal/resignation hits a held or shorted name, the brief reports the base rate for the matching comp set (founder-vacuum vs clean-succession), the 3-month and 1-year numbers, and what it means for the position (hold / no new short premium / earnings date as the next catalyst). Why: Jamie owns NVDA and asked for exactly this. Expected value: every future CEO event gets a base-rate bullet within the same brief — measured as brief coverage (share of CEO events with a base rate) and, over time, whether the comp-set call beat the unconditional average.
2. **Japan/carry bullet with differential + positioning (build first — Dec 17–18 BOJ meeting is the next catalyst).** When Japan PPI/CPI runs hot or BOJ hike odds move, the brief reports the US-Japan differential, CFTC yen positioning, the Aug 2024 precedent numbers, and the Sept 2026 counter-case. Why: the carry trade was a standing theme for 2 years and the desk flags it without explaining it. Expected value: the brief stops saying "Japan PPI hot" and starts saying "Dec hike odds up, differential X, shorts Y — unwind needs compression + a US weak print, per Aug 2024."
3. **Hot-CPI / Fed-surprise trigger (build second).** Same shape: prior hot-CPI prints (Nov 10: headline +0.4% vs +0.3% cons, core +0.3% for the third straight month) with 1-day/1-week S&P, 10Y, and VIX responses + Dec pricing moves. Why: CPI is the highest-frequency trigger in the brief and today it gets a verdict, not a base rate.
4. **Single-name earnings / guidance shock at a held name (build second).** Prior same-name earnings gaps (CRWV +24.7% Nov 11, MU +11.89%) with the 5-day follow-through rate. Why: the book is short premium into earnings; the base rate decides whether to hold premium through the print.
5. **AI-regulation / politics shock (build third — the Nov 5 crash driver).** Prior political AI shocks with 1-week/1-month S&P and data-center-name responses. Why: Nov 5 proved politics moves the tape, and the brief's first draft of it ("rotation, not risk-off") was wrong — a base rate would have caught it.

### Upstream data dependencies + maintenance
- Price history: live chain (Schwab+Yahoo merge) already in fildi; trigger studies run on fildi bars at build time, not on scraped snippets.
- Event dates: hand-collected, pre-registered per trigger in `research/trigger-library/<trigger>.md` (instance list + windows + comp-set rule) — pre-registration is the anti-gaming control: instances are fixed before the next event, never picked after.
- Positioning/macro: CFTC yen positioning (weekly, free), BOJ/Fed meeting calendars, Reuters economist polls — a small weekly sync, same pattern as `research/lseg/`.
- Maintenance: each trigger re-run quarterly (new instances appended, base rates recomputed); a trigger whose base rate flips sign on new data gets its bullet wording updated in the same commit. Owner: whoever owns the brief crons (Muse).

### Pass/fail bars (each trigger judged separately)
- Every trigger study: n ≥ 8 prior instances, base rates at 1d / 1w / 1m / 3mo, comp-set split stated with its rule (e.g. founder-vacuum vs clean-succession), sources named. Fewer than 8 instances → the trigger ships as "too few cases — no base rate," never as a verdict.
- Brief integration: the cron consults the library file for a firing trigger; a bullet that cites a trigger without its base rate is a FAIL of the brief, not of the library.
- Seed triggers (CEO scandal, Japan/carry) must be in the library before the next brief that needs them — NVDA earnings Tue Nov 17 and the Dec 17–18 BOJ meeting are the first live tests.
