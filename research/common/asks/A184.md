## Detail
**Tier 1: data already cached** (SPY minute bars, `optlab.minute_bars`, Jan→Oct 2026, from S048):
1. **Opening range breakout (ORB):** trade SPY/QQQ in the direction of the first 5-/15-min range break, stop at the range's other side, exit at the close. Evidence: Zarattini & Aziz (2023, SSRN) report strong 5-min ORB results on QQQ. Popular and testable.
2. **Intraday momentum:** the first half hour's return predicts the last half hour (Gao, Han, Li & Zhou 2018, JFE). Documented in the literature; cheap.
3. **Fair value gap (ICT/SMC):** a 3-bar gap gets "filled", then price reverses. Evidence is weak: a 2026 SSRN preprint (Mahadzva) found "no evidence ICT/SMC concepts work as literally taught", and in a random walk gaps were revisited at the same rate as random levels (83.4 % vs 83.5 %). Test = fill rate vs a random-level base rate first, then the trade. Expect a fail; cheap to show.

**Tier 2: daily data we have** (`lseg_research.daily_history` 2018→, IBES, earnings):
4. **Analyst revision / post-earnings drift:** buy after forward estimates or price targets go up (memory: expectation changes predict, cheapness doesn't). Strongest prior of the list.
5. **Time-series momentum / 200-day trend filter on SPY/QQQ** (Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012). Also a candidate as a put-book sizing dial.
6. **52-week-high proximity** (George & Hwang 2004).
7. **RSI(2) short-term mean reversion on index ETFs** (Connors). S025 found no bounce in single stocks; index level is a different question.
8. **Volatility squeeze / VCP breakout:** after realized vol hits a 6-month low, trade the breakout. Popular, little published evidence.

**Tier 3: build from what we have** (Jamie 10-19: "no data excuses"):
9. **Unusual options activity:** contract volume vs its own trailing volume, from Polygon daily option bars (2 years; `optlab.bars` has `v`) plus Polygon minute prints. Volume vs OI is added from the Schwab and Yahoo chain snapshots (OI on 18k+ rows since 10-08). Testable now on volume alone.
10. **Dealer gamma (GEX):** OI by strike from the Schwab + Yahoo daily chains (350 / 225 tickers, since 10-08). Two ways to start now: (a) a proxy that weights gamma by Polygon daily volume, testable on 2 years; (b) probe LSEG Workspace for historical OI per option RIC (offline sync).
11. **Real intraday spreads for ORB / FVG:** WRDS `taqm_2026` (millisecond trades + NBBO for stocks/ETFs) replaces Polygon trade prints for entry and exit realism.
