## Detail (pre-registered — thresholds fixed here, no re-cuts)
**States, monthly, point-in-time (known at month-end t):**
- Earnings breadth B(t) = % of universe names whose next-FY EPS mean rose > 1 % over 3 months minus % that fell > 1 %, **fpedats-matched**
  (same rule as `research/market-views/flows.py`), `wrds_research.ibes_statsum` 2016-01 → latest (949 names). ΔB = B(t) − B(t−3m).
- Cycle state: **upcycle** B > +10 and ΔB ≥ 0 · **late** B > 0 and ΔB < 0 · **downcycle** B < −10 and ΔB < 0 · **repair** B ≤ 0 and ΔB ≥ 0
  (anything left = nearest by B sign).
- Geo stress: GPR 30-day avg (`watchlist_sync.macro_daily` gprd_ma30) ≥ its expanding-window 90th percentile since 2000 · oil shock: WTI 3-month change ≥ +15 %.

**Tests**
1. Regime ID: SPY forward 6-month return and P(max drawdown ≥ 10 % within 6 m) by cycle state, 2016–2020 vs 2021→ halves.
   **Pass** = P(≥ 10 % DD) in downcycle ≥ upcycle + 15 pp in both halves.
2. Structures: S076 rows (CSP, jade lizard, covered call, short strangle) net return/trade and worst 5 % by cycle state and by geo stress,
   month-clustered t. **Pass** = a state shifts a structure's net by ≥ 0.25 %/trade with t ≥ 2 in both halves (2021–23 / 2024→).
3. Put band: S101 candidate rows (net real-fill bp/wk, worst 5 %) by cycle × S101 market state. **Pass** = within the same market state,
   the best band differs by cycle state and beats the engine's pick by ≥ 5 bp/wk, t ≥ 2, both halves (fit ≤ 2022, grade 2023→, as S101).
4. Placebo: shift B by 6 months forward (look-ahead) and 12 months back; a real effect should beat the lagged version.
