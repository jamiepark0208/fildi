Finish the Chronos-Bolt evaluation that stopped at an unfair test, and end it with a verdict: adopt as a research lane (with a settings catalog row) or drop the thread for good. A tested tool left in limbo — neither used nor dropped — is the failure this row closes.

## 1st-level analysis (what happened)
- Oct 26 VM test: chronos-bolt-small on CPU, SPY closes, 21-day rolling realized-vol target, 60-day holdout. Naive persistence RMSE 0.00564 / MASE 0.613 beat Chronos 0.00652 / 0.820; GARCH(1,1) far worse (0.27315 / 11.749). Verdict then: runs on CPU, has not earned a fildi job.
- The target was smooth (21-day rolling RV), which favors naive persistence by construction. That test answered "does Bolt beat naive on a smooth target" (no), not "does Bolt forecast anything useful."
- Weights cache (706M) and the Oct 26 protocol are on record; the builder re-creates the harness (the protocol, not any VM path, is the spec).

## 2nd-level analysis (why it matters)
- If Bolt beats naive on a less-smooth target, fildi gains a $0 CPU time-series forecaster for research lanes (vol, VRP gap) with no vendor, no key, no subscription.
- If it loses again, the thread closes with evidence instead of lingering as "maybe useful."
- Either way the row ends in a settings catalog row (adopted lane, or a recorded drop) — no silent limbo. That catalog row is acceptance criteria, not decoration.

## Use cases
- **UC1 — Next-day realized vol.** Target = tomorrow's 1-day RV, not 21-day rolling. Why: the trading-relevant horizon; a win here feeds vol-regime research (A314 context) and put-timing studies. Expected value: a documented beat-or-drop on the horizon that matters, not the smooth one.
- **UC2 — VRP gap.** Target = implied vol minus subsequently realized vol (the premium-harvest edge). Why: directly adjacent to the put-selling book; a forecaster that anticipates VRP compression/expansion is worth more than one that tracks smooth RV. Expected value: a research input to premium timing, or a clean drop.
- **UC3 — Short-window RV (5-day rolling).** Middle ground between UC1 noise and the old 21-day smoothness. Why: separates "wrong target" from "wrong model." Expected value: tells us whether the Oct 26 loss was target smoothness or model weakness.
