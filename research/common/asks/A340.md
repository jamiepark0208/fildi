## 1. Goal (Jamie's reel, distilled)

A build-log reel (QuantStack · AlgoFactory, Day 2) shows a genetic-algorithm factory that evolves intraday futures strategies (campaign generations over a genome lattice of indicator triggers/filters), vaults winners with IS/OOS + robustness gates, combines 21 uncorrelated strategies into one portfolio (avg member Sharpe 1.53 → combined 4.57), validates with walk-forward folds + Monte Carlo, and exports to NinjaTrader/MT5 for hands-off live trading. The software is closed — nothing to install. What transfers to fildi is the **method**: genetic search over strategy genomes + a portfolio combiner scored on return correlation, with honest IS/OOS reporting. This row is a [quant] study first, a research-lane build second, gated on the study passing. Research lane only — no live score input, no auto-trading, no futures.

## 2. Muse 1st-level analysis

Fildi has no genetic algorithm (Oct 28 audit) and no way to combine strategy legs into a portfolio — the Backtest Lab (A331) and the put-pnl harness (A270) test one strategy at a time. The reel's two portable ideas are (a) the search: evolve trigger/filter combinations against IS/OOS + fitness gates instead of hand-picking indicators; (b) the combiner: score a portfolio by how independently its members' equity curves move (1 − avg pairwise return correlation), not by how different the strategies look. The reel's own numbers prove both the promise and the trap: the 4.57 combined Sharpe is arithmetically sound (Muse's Monte Carlo re-run: 21 legs at Sharpe 1.53 and ρ≈0.07 gives 4.52), but 85% of the combined profit is in-sample and the visible legs decay OOS by a mean factor of 0.42 — so the honest OOS combined Sharpe is ~1.91, not 4.57. A fildi version must report the OOS-only number as the headline, or it repeats the reel's exact failure mode.

## 3. Muse 2nd-level analysis

- **The diversity definition is the stealable insight.** Their Grade A 97/100 rests on return correlation of equity curves, with worst pair 0.46 — a measurable, portable rule. Fildi's existing correlation work (A272 cleaning, A257 VaR tail-alignment) is about risk, not about selecting complementary legs. The combiner turns correlation from a risk input into a selection criterion.
- **Genetic search is a multiple-testing machine.** 571 curves per campaign, 30 generations, champion-picking — without strict gates this is overfitting with extra steps. The reel's own gates (IS/OOS split, robustness 81%, walk-forward 3/3 folds, Monte Carlo P(profit) 97% + p95 max DD) are the minimum viable discipline, and fildi's study must add one more: the OOS-only combined Sharpe reported separately from the full-history number, with the IS share of profit stated.
- **The put book is the natural first universe, not futures.** Fildi's legs are put-selling structures and indicator signals (A336's S154 playbook already maps structures × sides × holds walk-forward). A GA that searches put-structure × signal × DTE genomes on the put-pnl harness is directly comparable to the live engine — the same packet pattern A270 established.

## 4. Use cases (concrete, with why + expected value)

- **UC1 — Combine the put book's legs instead of ranking them one by one.** Today the Model List ranks single puts; nothing measures whether two top-ranked puts are the same bet twice. A combiner that scores candidate legs by pairwise return correlation (their diversity definition) and reports the combined OOS Sharpe of the top-N uncorrelated legs tells Jamie whether adding a second leg actually diversifies or just doubles the same exposure. Expected value: fewer correlated adds to the live book; every new leg justified by what it adds to the portfolio, not its standalone rank.
- **UC2 — Genetic search over put-structure × signal genomes on the put-pnl harness.** Instead of hand-testing one structure at a time (S152/S154 pattern), a GA explores combinations — structure × indicator signal × DTE × side — with IS/OOS + walk-forward + Monte Carlo gates, and returns the surviving legs with their OOS decay reported. Expected value: the search covers combinations no human would hand-test, and the gates (not the GA) decide what survives; a leg that only wins in-sample dies in the study, never reaches the book.
- **UC3 — A portable validation template for every future study.** Walk-forward folds (profitable 3/3) + Monte Carlo P(profit) + p95 max DD + separate OOS-only headline becomes the standard gate for Backtest Lab studies, replacing ad-hoc holdouts. Expected value: every later study is judged by the same bar, and the reel's failure mode (full-history headline, IS-dominated profit) becomes a checkable violation instead of a recurring surprise.

## 8. Pass / fail bars

- **P1 (math reproduces):** on fildi data, the combiner's reported combined Sharpe matches an independent recomputation from member returns within 0.05, and the implied avg pairwise ρ is reported alongside. Fail = mismatch, or ρ unreported.
- **P2 (honest OOS):** the study reports the OOS-only combined Sharpe and the IS share of combined profit as separate headline numbers; the OOS combined Sharpe beats the best single-leg OOS Sharpe by ≥0.3, or the study closes as failed validation. Fail = full-history headline only, or no OOS beat.
- **P3 (decay disclosed):** every surviving leg reports IS Sharpe, OOS Sharpe, and decay ratio; mean decay ≤0.6 triggers a written explanation of why the legs still merit the combiner. Fail = any surviving leg without an OOS number.
- **P4 (gates hold):** walk-forward profitable folds 3/3, Monte Carlo P(profit) ≥90%, p95 max DD reported. Fail = any gate missed without a written, reviewed exception.
- **P5 (no live leakage):** zero changes to live scores, live rules, or positions from any step; the GA never trades, never files, never touches the book. Fail = one live touch. Stop rule: any live-score or live-money effect closes the row.
- **Stop rules:** Step 1 finds no surviving legs → close as failed validation, no build. Any attempt to export to a live-trading platform (NinjaTrader/MT5/cTrader) → STOP, out of scope for fildi, close that step.
