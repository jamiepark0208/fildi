## Detail
1. **Fetch (≈ 9 h at 60/min, run alone on LSE — never two LSE jobs):** names = S175 long-call universe top 150 by $ volume (s184 picks the same list). Mondays 2025-10-06 → latest. Per name-Monday: monthly expiry nearest 30 DTE; strikes = listed strikes nearest the 25Δ put, 25Δ call and ATM (Δ from spot + lseg iv30; `list_put_strikes` / `/v3/pairs` for listed strikes). One export per contract over its life (daily OHLCV of trades). Store whole responses (vendor_raw, A266). Resume from watermark; `--backfill` only for the history.
2. **Build:** per name-Monday, IV from the trade close (European BS, T-bill r) → rr25 = IV(25Δ call) − IV(25Δ put), smirk = IV(25Δ put) − IV(ATM), cw = IV(ATM call) − IV(ATM put). Drop contracts with no trade that day (stale) — report the share dropped.
3. **Test (pre-registered, write before looking):**
   - Puts: within-Monday rank IC of smirk and rr25 on the S003-grain 30-DTE 0.20Δ put ret_ra (weekly entries), and an increment over IV/RV20 (partial IC). Pass: |IC| ≥ 0.03, t ≥ 2 over non-overlapping 4-week origins, same sign both halves.
   - Calls: S175 long calls (0.50Δ 30 DTE) top fifth by cw (Cremers–Weinbaum direction) vs all calls that Monday, and added to the S177 score. Pass: +$ per $ debit with month t ≥ 2, both halves.
   - n_indep ≈ 13 months / 4 wk ≈ 14 → expect UNDERPOWERED; label it so, and keep it as a forward ledger (A308 style) if the sign is right in both halves.
