# A92 — [quant] Assignment-aware track record (2-week exit rule + per-stock drill-down)

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor (via Claude [quant] review)
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Severity:** high (the track record currently hides the biggest losses)

## Context
Jamie asked (Oct 14): the track record tab shows an ever-upward cumulative P&L — are assignment
losses tracked? No. `settleRec()` in `model-track-record.ts` settles synthetic short recs at expiry
intrinsic (`pnlPerShare = premium − intrinsic`) and stops. An assigned put's stock leg is never
modeled. He proposed the fix: assume a 2-week post-assignment window — sell assigned stock (put
assignment) at the 2-week max, buy back (call assignment) at the 2-week min. He also wants
per-stock cumulative P&L via clickable ticker rows. Inventory completed Oct 15.

## What exists today
- Track record grades **synthetic model recs**, not Jamie's actual trades; settlement is
  premium-minus-intrinsic at expiry; zero assignment handling.
- UI (`track-record.tsx`): cumulative P&L AreaChart + per-ticker table (wins/losses, avg P&L,
  Sharpe). **Rows not clickable — no drill-down.**
- Real assignment data EXISTS and is unused: `portfolio_transactions` has
  `assignment_buy`/`assignment_sell` rows; `portfolio_ticker_earnings` has per-ticker stock P&L.
- `scripts/watchlist_sync/wheel.py` already computes per-ticker options + stock realized +
  unrealized + dividends ("an option win rate says nothing about the stock you got assigned") —
  but it's an offline stdout script, not wired into the UI.

## What to build
1. **Assignment leg in `settleRec()`**: ITM at expiry ⇒ assigned (no historical option prices exist,
   so this is the only rule). Short put assigned → long stock at strike; exit at max close over the
   next 10 trading days (`prices_historical`). Short call assigned → short stock at strike; cover at
   10-day min. Book stock-leg P&L separately from premium P&L so both are visible.
2. **Report both numbers**: premium P&L and assignment-inclusive P&L side by side; the cumulative
   chart gets a toggle (premium-only vs all-in). The "managed" variant keeps its −2× stop on the
   premium leg; the stock leg follows the 2-week rule.
3. **Clickable ticker rows**: row click → per-stock detail (cumulative P&L curve, trade list,
   wheel total = options + stock + dividends). Promote `wheel.py` logic into the track-record API.
4. **Actual-trades view**: wire the real `assignment_buy`/`assignment_sell` + `portfolio_ticker_earnings`
   data into the same per-stock view, so Jamie sees model recs and his real book side by side.
