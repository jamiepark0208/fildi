## 1st-level analysis

Thursday proved the gap math: a put sold 10% OTM on SMH was ITM by the close; on single semi names, deep ITM. Standard 5–10% OTM weekly strikes were not enough in one session. Premium looked rich because realized vol was running hot, not because of a free edge — and the curve agreed: VIX9D (27.18 Fri) still above the 30-day VIX, backwardation day 2 (since 2010, only 7.6% of days). The tradable lesson is not "don't sell premium" but "don't sell it blind into a stacked event window while the curve is inverted."

## 2nd-level analysis

The guard belongs at order entry / position review, not inside the score. Scores rank names; the guard answers a different question — is *this* expiry, *this* week, a week to be short gamma at all? Two inputs already exist in fildi (vol data, earnings calendar per A356's dated-calendar fix); the missing piece is the rule that combines them and a strike-distance minimum that widens after a name prints a −10% day. If the guard had existed Thu Nov 5, every new AI/semi/neocloud short would have carried a hard warn, and the Nov 6 book review would have flagged nothing new — the book was already positioned, expiries already set.

## Use cases

1. **Monday open, new short put.** Jamie opens a short put; guard fires "CPI Tue + CRWV earnings inside expiry, VIX backwardated" → he widens the strike, shrinks size, or waits until Wed Nov 11. Why: one print can gap through a normal strike; the guard prices that in before the fill.
2. **Book review before a stacked week.** Guard flags open positions expiring Nov 13 with events inside the window → roll/close decision before the print, not after the gap. Why: the Nov 6 expiries expired worthless by luck of direction (IREN +20%, OKLO +22.7% that day); the same stack the other way would have assigned.
3. **Auto-clear after the stack.** Guard clears when the curve is back to contango and no event sits inside the window. Why: a guard that never clears gets ignored; the clear condition is part of the feature.

## Detail

- **Inputs:** VIX term structure (VIX, VIX9D, VIX3M — verify source already in fildi, else add); macro calendar (CPI/PPI/FOMC/NFP dates); earnings calendar per holding (A356 dated-calendar fix); position expiry window; per-name worst 1-day drop, trailing 30 days.
- **Rule — guard fires when BOTH hold:** (a) term structure backwardated (VIX > VIX3M, or VIX9D > VIX); (b) a macro print or a holding's earnings lands inside the position's expiry window.
- **Severity:** hard warn for single-name AI/semi/neocloud (the names that printed −10% days); soft warn for index premium.
- **Strike-distance minimum (when guard fires):** for names with a −10% day in the last 5 sessions, minimum OTM distance = 1.5× the name's worst 1-day drop in the last 30 days (15% floor for the book's high-vol names: IREN, OKLO, RGTI, OPEN, ONDS, CRWV). Index: widen by 1.5× the recent daily range vs the usual strike.
- **Display:** guard badge on the options view + a line in the book brief / pre-open brief. One line: what fired, what event, what strike minimum applies. No new tab.
- **Backtest the rule** over 2025–2026 short-premium history in the fildi DB (replay: would the guard have fired on each position that later lost >2× credit?).

## Expected results (quantitative)

- Guard fires on ≥80% of historical positions that later lost >2× credit.
- False-positive rate (fired, but position expired worthless) measured and reported; target <40%.
- No more than 1 in 4 trading weeks fully blocked (guard must not become a permanent "don't trade" sign).
- On a replayed Thu Nov 5 setup, the strike minimums keep the book's strikes out of the gap zone on the Thursday names (SMH −11.2%, AMD −17%, MU −17.5%).

## Pass / fail

- Guard fires on a replayed Thu Nov 5 setup (backwardation + CPI/earnings in window) and stays silent on a replayed quiet week (contango, no event in window).
- Strike minimums as specified above; backtest numbers (hit rate on >2×-credit losers, false-positive rate, weeks blocked) reported in the file.
- Claude [quant] review of the backwardation definition and the 1.5× multiplier before build.

## Downstream impact

Options view (guard badge), book brief / pre-open brief (guard line), position sizing (size = risk ÷ max loss already uses max loss; the guard adds the pre-trade gate and the widened strike minimum, which changes the max-loss input).
