# A62: Brief staleness guard (A45 gap)
**From → to:** Muse → Cursor · **Date:** 2026-10-11 · **Status:** open

## Ask
The Daily News brief-first pipeline serves a stale Muse brief as current with no staleness check — Monday morning shows Friday's brief and banner levels as if fresh. The A45 spec required a staleness check that was never built.

## Detail
`loadMuseBrief()` in `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/desk-daily-news.ts` reads `commentary/latest-am|pm.md` with no date validation. Before Monday's 6:10 AM brief lands, the route serves Friday's PM brief; banner levels can be 3 days old. The panel (`daily-news-panel.tsx`) shows the brief filename with no date or staleness signal, so a stale brief is indistinguishable from a fresh one.
Fix: parse the brief's date (prefer the dated `YYYY-MM-DD-am|pm.md` file over `latest-`), show "brief as of <date>" in the panel, add a stale badge when the brief predates the current session, and prefer "no brief yet" over a stale brief on Monday pre-brief. Severity: degraded. Plan-only; no score changes.
