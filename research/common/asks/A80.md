# A80 — Thesis tracker (morning call vs evening outcome, scored)

**Status:** open
**From:** Muse → Cursor
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Severity:** medium

## Context
Jamie asked (Oct 14) that the market recaps wire into fildi. Idea 2 of 3: track every morning-brief
thesis against the evening outcome, score it, and show the hit rate over time. This is the
calibration dataset for the brief itself — and a direct input to A74's forecast-scoring discipline
(Brier-style scoring of stated theses).

## What to build
1. **Thesis extraction**: each morning brief's key calls (directional, level, event) stored as
   structured records: {date, thesis, direction, target/level, horizon, confidence}.
2. **Evening grading**: at recap time, each open thesis graded right / wrong / mixed against realized
   outcomes. Grading rules deterministic and documented (no vibes).
3. **UI**: "Thesis tracker" panel — recent theses with grades, rolling hit-rate (30d), calibration
   view (high-confidence calls: what % were right?). One line per thesis, newest first.
4. **Backend**: new table `brief_theses`; grading job runs with the evening recap; aggregates cached.
   Manual override for the grader when the rule misfires (logged).

## Acceptance
- [ ] Every morning brief since launch has structured theses stored.
- [ ] Evening recap grades prior theses; panel shows rolling accuracy.
- [ ] Calibration view distinguishes high- vs low-confidence calls.
- [ ] No silent failures: ungraded theses alert after 24h.
