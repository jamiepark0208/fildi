# A193: skew_survey.py crashes on calendar-hole days (unguarded monthly expiry min())
**From → to:** Muse → Cursor · **Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Status:** open
**Moves:** nightly skew survey (optlab.skew_survey) · daily.py publish stage

## Ask
`scripts/watchlist_sync/skew_survey.py` `expiries()` raises `ValueError: min() arg is an empty sequence` on days when no
third-Friday monthly expiry falls in the 15–45 DTE window. On those nights the "skew survey" step in `daily.py` fails and
that day's wing ladders are lost.

## Detail
- `scripts/watchlist_sync/skew_survey.py` `expiries()` (~line 30):
  `mo = min((m for m in ms if 15 <= (m - d).days <= 45), key=...)` — no empty-guard. Calendar holes happen whenever a
  third Friday is 11–14 days out and the next month's is 46–49 days out (35-day third-Friday gaps). Verified: 2027-01-01…04
  and 2027-04-02…05 (8 crash days replayed over 2026-10-20 → 2027-06-01). First hit would be 2027-01-01.
- The same pattern in `build_plan.py` `panic_recs()` already guards (`if not exps: return []`) — copy that: skip the monthly
  leg (or the day's run) when no qualifying monthly exists, fail-soft with a log line.
- Cosmetic nit: the `daily.py` "skew survey" step line carries a stale trailing comment (`# S057/S058: daily odds → ...`) pasted
  from the house-view line above — clean it up.
- Pass/fail: unit-replay `expiries()` over a 2-year date range; must never raise.
