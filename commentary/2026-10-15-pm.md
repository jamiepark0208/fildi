# Evening Recap — Thursday, October 15, 2026 (post-close)

**Banner:** S&P 7,744.06 +0.33% | Dow 52,177.69 +1.08% | Nasdaq 26,713.03 +0.08% | 10Y 5.09% (−10bp) | WTI ~$97.5 (+2.2%) | VIX 16.46 (−2.4%)

## What moved today
- **Hot PPI kept the Fed on the hook.** Sep PPI 1.3% m/m (vs 0.6% est), 6.3% y/y (vs 5.5%); core PPI 0.6% m/m — biggest gain in five months — and 5.0% y/y. October-hike odds eased to 29% anyway (from 29–37%); December at 88%.
- **The consumer offset the inflation scare.** Retail sales +0.8% (double est; ex-vehicle +0.9%), claims 199k — the demand leg is what let defensives run instead of the market cowering.
- **Rotation, not rally.** Dow +1.1% (+556) led by NKE/CRM/HD; Nasdaq erased a 1%+ hole to close +0.08% at 26,713. Healthcare set record highs; staples extended their 8%+ October run. Tech kept bleeding underneath: SOX down again (8th straight), memory names crushed on the CXMT announcement (SK Hynix −11%, Samsung −9.5%), CRWV −7% to $61.25 with 5y CDS at 1,118bps.
- **Bonds and vol faded the panic.** 10Y closed 5.09% (−10bp on the day) after spiking to 5.33% post-CPI yesterday; VIX slid to 16.46 (−2.4%) from 17.7 intraday. Dollar softened: DXY −0.5% to 102.02.
- **Asia took the real damage.** KOSPI −7.6% with a circuit-breaker halt; Nikkei −4.5%; Shanghai −2.4%; Hang Seng −1.5%; Taiex ~−3%. Europe held firmer: FTSE +1.1%, DAX +0.3%; Ericsson +9.9% on a smaller-than-expected Q3 profit drop, Pernod Ricard +3.2%.

## Breadth and leadership
- Defensive leadership: healthcare record highs, staples +8% in October; energy firmed with WTI +2.2% to ~$97.5.
- Safe-haven scoreboard favored metals over crypto: gold futures +1.8% to $4,181.30, silver +1.6% to $56.78; bitcoin slipped to ~$80.3k (−2%).
- Rates context: 10Y 5.09% (−10bp), 30Y ~5.40% (−13bp from the morning), 2Y ~4.69%; bull flattening intact, 2s10s near 35bp. 10Y is in three-week-low territory despite the hot PPI.
- Small caps joined the rotation rather than the rout; the Dow closed above 52,000, which confirms the rotation is absorbing tech outflows instead of spreading into a broad selloff.

## What changed vs the morning thesis
- **Got right:** hot PPI as the day's arbiter (1.3%/6.3% vs 0.6%/5.5% est); the Asia meltdown framing the open; defensive rotation over broad panic; expiration-day discipline flagged for today's close.
- **Surprised to the upside:** Nasdaq futures were −1.2% premarket and the Nasdaq closed +0.08%; the S&P erased early losses for +0.33%. The morning read had risk-off pinning the tape down; instead the strong retail/claims data and the defensive bid lifted the close, and VIX faded hard from 17.7 intraday to 16.46.
- **New after the close:** AA beat on EPS ($1.82 vs $1.51 est; rev $3.7B light of $3.8B) — +2.3% after hours to $41.35. IBKR reported — roughly flat-to-down after hours on a slight miss. JBHT −2%+ on an earnings miss despite stronger revenue. Varonis +2%+ on Proofpoint's $6.3B all-cash acquisition.
- **Still to come tonight:** Warsh with Georgieva at the IMF at 11:30p ET — the last Fed voice before Saturday's quiet period.

## Book impact
