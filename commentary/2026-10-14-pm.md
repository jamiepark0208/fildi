# Evening Recap — Wednesday, October 14, 2026 (post-close)

**Banner:** S&P 7,718.94 −1.11% | Dow 51,621.48 −0.09% | Nasdaq 26,692.87 −1.88% | 10Y ~5.19% (−3bp) | Brent ~$106.50 (+~0.8%) | VIX 16.81 (+10.1%)

## What moved today
- **Hot CPI repriced the Fed.** Sep headline 3.7% y/y (vs 3.6% est, 3.4% prior), 0.6% m/m; core 2.6% y/y (vs 2.5% est), 0.3% m/m. Fastest pace of increase since April. October hike odds jumped to 37–40% from 22% overnight.
- **AI leverage got smoked.** Oracle −8.8%, CoreWeave −15%, Nebius −10%, Dell −11.4% and HPE −8.2% after a Barclays downgrade. SOX −4.3%, its 6th straight down day — worst run since May 2025.
- **Yields spiked, then faded.** 10Y jumped to ~5.29% (briefly over 5.32%) on the print, then eased back to ~5.19% (−3bp) by the close. 2Y +12bp to 4.92%, highest since Sep 29.
- **Bank earnings split the tape.** BAC −8.2% on a revenue miss plus a $1.2B regulatory fine; STT soft on the top line; MS headline beat with mixed internals; BLK clean beat.
- **One bright AI data point landed after hours.** Anthropic's closed-door investor day disclosed Q3 revenue of $16.8B (+46% q/q), adjusted-profitable and cash-flow positive, with a November IPO on the runway. Futures sat flat-to-down after hours; TSM reports tomorrow.

## Breadth and leadership
- Ugly cap-weighted, healthy underneath: only 8 of 11 S&P sectors fell, but 245 names rose vs 256 fell. Tech's average constituent −3.2%; staples +2.4% and health +2.4% led. Defensive rotation, not broad panic.
- Dow −0.09% vs Nasdaq −1.88% — the widest Nasdaq–Dow gap since July 2. QQQ volume ran ~49% above its 20-day average.
- Small caps joined the pain: Russell 2000 −1.93%. VIX +10% to ~16.8. Gold −1.4% to $4,119; bitcoin −2% to ~$81,700. Dollar firmed ~0.3% on the data.
- Overseas shrugged: Stoxx 600 +0.4%, DAX +0.2%, CAC +0.6%, FTSE +0.7%. Asia firmer pre-CPI (Nikkei +0.3%, Kospi +0.8%); China's Sep exports beat big (+36.3% y/y) while PPI ran hot at 5.0%.

## What changed vs the morning thesis
- **Got right:** CPI as the day's arbiter; banks mixed with BAC the laggard; chips still bleeding (6th day).
- **Got wrong:** both CPI legs came in hot (3.7% / 2.6% vs 3.6% / 2.5% est). Morning's "yields easing" flipped intraday — the spike, not the ease, was the story until the afternoon fade. October-hike odds nearly doubled to 37–40%.
- **New:** the AI selloff's scale (Oracle/CoreWeave/neoclouds, Barclays server-maker downgrades) — the morning framed chips as the weak link; the damage spread to AI infrastructure broadly. Beige Book confirmed the energy-inflation channel: oil cost pass-through in all 12 districts. Apple's $5T market-cap close (+0.5%) on a −1.9% Nasdaq day is the lone bullish footnote.

## Book impact
- **Premium bid is back.** VIX +10% on a −1.1% S&P day with PPI/retail sales tomorrow 8:30a ET and an Oct 27–28 FOMC ahead — keep new short premium light into the morning prints; the seller's window is post-print, not pre.
- **Memory semis are un-sellable here.** SOX down 6 straight (MU −4.1%, AMD −5.7%); the CXMT supply story is unresolved — no short puts on the cohort, or widen hard.
- **AI-infrastructure names are gap-risk, not premium opportunities.** ORCL/CRWV/DELL-type moves are −9% to −15% in a day; rich premium is not compensation for that. Index put spreads beat concentrated tech shorts.
- **Post-earnings IV crush worked on the clean beats** (BLK) but BAC −8.2% shows why size stays small on idiosyncratic bank risk. Energy held (+0.8%) — a short-energy-vol leg is the only sector offering compression today.
